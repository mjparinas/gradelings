Similar shapes have the same shape, but can be different sizes. One is an enlargement of the other.
Everything is multiplied by the same number. This is the scale factor. Here we multiply by two.
A small rectangle is three long and two tall. With a scale factor of two, it becomes six long and four tall.
Scale also works on maps. If one centimetre stands for ten kilometres, five centimetres stand for fifty.
To use a scale, multiply or divide by the same number for every length.
