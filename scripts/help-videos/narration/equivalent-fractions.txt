Equivalent fractions look different, but they show the same amount.
Here is one half. The bar has two equal parts, and one part is shaded.
Now cut every part in half. The bar has four parts, and two are shaded. The shaded amount did not change.
To get from one half to two fourths, multiply the top and the bottom by two.
Always multiply the top and the bottom by the same number. Then the fraction keeps its size.
