Welcome to the parent area. It is a grown-ups-only space, behind a PIN that only you know.
Overview shows how each child is doing this week: time, questions answered and how many were right on the first try.
Under each child you can see how every subject is going, and tap Full report for the details.
Reports has charts, strengths, and a table of every unit.
Children is where you add or edit your children, and Settings lets you tune each child's experience.
Account is for signing in, syncing between devices, your PIN and the read-aloud voice.
