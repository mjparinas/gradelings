Each child has their own settings. Open Settings and pick the child at the top.
The daily goal sets how many minutes of learning fill their progress bar on the home screen.
Subjects lets you choose what shows up in lessons, Adventure and the Daily Challenge. French is optional and off by default.
Arcade games are earned with learning time. You can change the ratio, allow free play, or turn games off.
Changes save automatically and sync to your other devices.
