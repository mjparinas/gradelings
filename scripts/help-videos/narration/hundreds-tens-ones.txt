A three digit number is made of hundreds, tens and ones.
The three is in the hundreds place. It means three hundreds. That is three hundred.
The four is in the tens place. It means four tens, which is forty.
The seven is in the ones place. It means seven ones.
Put them together. Three hundred plus forty plus seven makes three hundred forty-seven.
Try another. Two hundreds, five tens and nine ones. That is two hundred, plus fifty, plus nine, which makes two hundred fifty-nine.
