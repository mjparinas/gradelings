To add decimals, line up the decimal points. Let's add three point four and two point seven five.
Write them in a column, with the decimal points straight. Add a zero so both have two places.
Add the hundredths. Zero plus five is five. Then the tenths. Four plus seven is eleven. Write one, and carry one.
Then the ones. Three plus two plus the carried one is six.
So three point four plus two point seven five is six point one five. Keep the decimal point in line.
