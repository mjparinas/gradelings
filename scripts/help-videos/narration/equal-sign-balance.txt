The equal sign means both sides are the same amount. Think of a balance.
Three plus two is five. Five is five. So three plus two equals five.
Now look at four plus one. Four plus one is five too. So both sides are equal.
What about three plus two, and six? Three plus two is five. Six is more. So they are not equal.
To check, work out each side. If they match, it is equal.
