Surface area is the total area of all the faces of a shape. Think of the wrapping paper.
A box has six faces, in three matching pairs. Say it is four by three by two.
The top and bottom are four by three. That is twelve each, so twenty-four for the pair.
The front and back are four by two. That is eight each, so sixteen for the pair. The two sides are three by two, six each, so twelve.
Add the three pairs. Twenty-four plus sixteen plus twelve is fifty-two square units.
