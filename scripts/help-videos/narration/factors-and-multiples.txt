A factor is a number that multiplies with another number to make a product. Let's find the factors of twelve.
Twelve counters can make a row of twelve. One times twelve is twelve. So one and twelve are factors.
They can make two rows of six. Two times six is twelve. So two and six are factors.
They can make three rows of four. Three times four is twelve. So three and four are factors.
The factors of twelve are one, two, three, four, six and twelve.
Multiples go the other way. They are the numbers in a times table. The multiples of four are four, eight, twelve, sixteen and so on.
