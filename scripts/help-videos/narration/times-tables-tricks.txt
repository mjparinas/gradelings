Times tables get easier with tricks. Let's learn one for fives and one for nines.
Multiples of five always end in zero or five. Five, ten, fifteen, twenty, twenty-five, thirty.
For five times six, think of half of ten times six. Ten times six is sixty. Half of sixty is thirty.
For nines, multiply by ten and then take away the number. Nine times six is ten times six, which is sixty, take away six. That is fifty-four.
Here is a check. The digits of every nine fact add up to nine. Fifty-four has a five and a four, and five plus four is nine.
