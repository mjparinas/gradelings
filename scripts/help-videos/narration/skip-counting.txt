Skip counting means counting by the same jump each time.
Let's count by twos. Two, four, six, eight, ten. Each hop is two.
Now count by fives. Five, ten, fifteen, twenty. Each hop is five.
And count by tens. Ten, twenty, thirty, forty. Each hop is ten.
To find what comes next in a pattern, look at two neighbours and see how much the number grows. Ten to twenty is a jump of ten, so the next number is forty.
