A fraction tells us about equal parts of a whole.
Here is a pizza cut into four equal parts. Equal means every part is the same size.
Let's shade one part. One out of the four parts is shaded.
We write that as one fourth. The bottom number says how many equal parts there are in all. The top number says how many of those parts we are talking about.
Now shade three parts. That is three fourths. The bottom number is still four, because the pizza still has four equal parts.
Remember: the bottom number counts all the equal parts. The top number counts the parts we pick.
