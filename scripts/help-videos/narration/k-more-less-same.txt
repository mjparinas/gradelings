Let's compare two groups. Which group has more?
Count each group. The top has five. The bottom has three.
Five is more than three. So the top group has more. The bottom group has fewer.
Sometimes the groups match. Four and four. Line them up, and nothing is left over.
When they match, they are the same.
Now you try. Count each group, and say which has more, which has fewer, or if they are the same.
