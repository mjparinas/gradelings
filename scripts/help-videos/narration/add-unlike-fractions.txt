To add fractions, the pieces must be the same size. Let's add one half and one third.
Halves and thirds are different sizes. We need a common denominator. Six works for both, because two and three both go into six.
Change each fraction to sixths. One half is three sixths, because we multiply the top and bottom by three.
One third is two sixths, because we multiply the top and bottom by two.
Now the pieces match, so we add the tops. Three sixths plus two sixths is five sixths. The bottom stays six.
