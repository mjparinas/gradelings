A discount takes a percent off a price. Say something costs fifty, and it is twenty percent off.
An easy start is ten percent. To find ten percent, divide by ten. Fifty divided by ten is five.
Twenty percent is double ten percent. Double five is ten. So the discount is ten.
Now take the discount off the price. Fifty minus ten is forty. That is the sale price.
Tax and tips work the same way, but you add the amount instead of taking it away.
