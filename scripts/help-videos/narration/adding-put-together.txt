Adding means putting groups together.
Here are three blue squares and two orange squares.
Put them together and count them all. One, two, three, four, five.
Three plus two equals five. The plus sign means put together. The equals sign means is the same as.
Here is a quick trick. Start at the bigger number, three, and count on two more. Four, five.
Try one yourself. What is two plus four? Start at the bigger number, four, and count on two. Five, six. The answer is six.
