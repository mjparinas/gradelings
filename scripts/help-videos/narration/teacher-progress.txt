The Progress tab shows every student and every unit you've assigned, with their level.
Each cell shows the level, the percent right on the first try, and how many questions the student has answered.
Use Sort by to put the students who need help, or the most active students, first.
Print a class summary, or download everything as a spreadsheet. It always says it reflects practice, not a report-card mark. You decide proficiency.
Below the table, What to look at next names the units worth a quick reteach and the students to check in with, and why. You can assign extra practice to them in one tap.
You can also set a weekly question goal for the whole class. Students see one shared bar with counts only: no names and no ranking.
