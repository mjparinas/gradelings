To compare fractions, think about how big the pieces are and how many we have.
When the bottom numbers match, the pieces are the same size. So more pieces is more. Three eighths is bigger than two eighths.
When the top numbers match, look at the bottom numbers. More parts in the whole means smaller pieces.
One half is bigger than one fourth, because a half piece is bigger than a fourth piece.
Same bottom number, compare the tops. Same top number, compare the bottoms, and the smaller bottom number is the bigger fraction.
