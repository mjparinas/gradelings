Rounding means finding the nearest friendly number.
Let's round four thousand six hundred twenty-eight to the nearest hundred. First find the hundreds on each side. They are four thousand six hundred and four thousand seven hundred.
Now look at the digit one place to the right of the hundreds. That is the tens digit. It is a two.
If that digit is zero to four, round down. If it is five to nine, round up.
Two is less than five, so we round down. Four thousand six hundred twenty-eight rounds to four thousand six hundred.
