A clock has two hands. The short hand shows the hour.
The long hand shows the minutes. When it points straight up at the twelve, it is o'clock. This clock says three o'clock.
When the long hand points straight down at the six, it is half past. That is thirty minutes. This clock says half past three.
Each number stands for five minutes. Count by fives to the three. Five, ten, fifteen. This clock says quarter past three.
To tell time, look at the short hand for the hour first. Then look at the long hand for the minutes.
