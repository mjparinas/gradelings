Multiplying with negative numbers follows a pattern. First, two positive numbers. Three times four is twelve.
Three times negative four means three groups of negative four. That is negative twelve.
Negative three times four is also negative twelve. The order does not matter.
And a negative times a negative is positive. Negative three times negative four is positive twelve.
Here is the rule. If the signs are the same, the answer is positive. If the signs are different, the answer is negative.
Dividing works the same way. Negative twelve divided by three is negative four.
