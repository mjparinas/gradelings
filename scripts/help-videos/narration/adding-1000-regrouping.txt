To add three digit numbers, line up the ones, the tens and the hundreds.
Start with the ones. Eight plus seven makes fifteen. Write the five and carry the one ten.
Now the tens. Five plus six plus the carried one makes twelve. Write the two and carry the one hundred.
Last, the hundreds. Two plus one plus the carried one makes four.
The answer is four hundred twenty-five. We can check with an estimate: two hundred sixty plus one hundred seventy is about four hundred thirty.
