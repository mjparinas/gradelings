A square number is what you get when you multiply a number by itself.
Picture it as a square of tiles. Four rows of four tiles make sixteen.
Sixteen is a perfect square, because the tiles make a square exactly.
The square root asks the question backwards. What number times itself makes sixteen? It is four. So the square root of sixteen is four.
The first perfect squares are one, four, nine, sixteen and twenty-five. It helps to know them.
