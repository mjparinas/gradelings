Slope tells us how steep a line is. We measure it as rise over run.
Pick two points on the line. The run is how far we go across. The rise is how far we go up.
Say we go across four, and up two. The rise is two. The run is four.
Slope is rise divided by run. Two divided by four is one half.
A bigger slope means a steeper line. A negative slope means the line goes down.
