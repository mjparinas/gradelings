To subtract bigger numbers, we work one place at a time. Let's find fifty-two minus twenty-seven.
Start with the ones. Two minus seven? We can't take seven away from two.
So we regroup. We borrow one ten from the five tens. Now there are four tens, and the two ones become twelve ones.
Now we can subtract the ones. Twelve minus seven is five.
Then the tens. Four tens minus two tens is two tens.
Put the parts together. Twenty plus five makes twenty-five. Check it by adding. Twenty-five plus twenty-seven is fifty-two.
