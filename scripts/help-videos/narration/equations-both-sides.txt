Some equations have x on both sides. Let's solve three x plus two equals x plus ten.
First, get the x terms together. Take one x away from both sides.
Next, get the plain numbers together. Take two away from both sides.
Now divide both sides by two. Two x divided by two is x. Eight divided by two is four.
Always check. Put four in for x. Three times four plus two is fourteen. Four plus ten is also fourteen. It works.
