Welcome to Gradelings for teachers. Let's set up your first class. It takes about a minute.
On the Your classes page, type a class name, pick the grade, and choose your province or state. Then tap Create class.
If you teach two neighbouring grades, choose a second grade, and your class can use units from both.
Every class has a class code. Families can use it to link their own child, and you only ever see first names, grade and practice results for the units you assign.
Along the top are five tabs: Progress, Homework, Live, Students and Settings. Progress is where you'll look every day.
If you'd like a weekly summary, tick Email me every Monday. It lists who practised, how homework is going, and who to check in with.
