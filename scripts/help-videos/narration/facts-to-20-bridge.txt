Here is a trick for adding past ten. Make a ten first.
Eight needs two more to make ten. So split the five into two and three.
Start at eight. Add two to reach ten.
Then add the three that is left. Ten plus three makes thirteen.
So eight plus five is thirteen. Make a ten, and then add what is left.
Try it with nine plus four. Nine needs one more to make ten. Take one from the four, and three is left. Ten plus three is thirteen.
