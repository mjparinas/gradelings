Every two digit number is made of tens and ones.
Look at the three. It is in the tens place, so it means three tens. Each stick holds ten.
Now look at the four. It is in the ones place, so it means four ones.
Three tens is thirty. Four ones is four. Thirty plus four makes thirty-four.
So the place of a digit tells you its value. Look at the tens first, and then the ones.
