An exponent tells us how many times to multiply a number by itself.
Two to the power of three is eight, because we multiply three twos.
What happens when we multiply powers with the same base? Take two to the power of three, times two to the power of two.
Write them out. Three twos, times two more twos. That is five twos multiplied together.
So we just add the exponents. Three plus two is five. Two to the power of five is thirty-two.
