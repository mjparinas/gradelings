When a problem has more than one operation, we follow a set order so everyone gets the same answer.
The order is: brackets first. Then multiply and divide, from left to right. Then add and subtract, from left to right.
In three plus four times two, multiply first. Four times two is eight. Then add: three plus eight is eleven.
Brackets change the order. In bracket three plus four bracket, times two, do the brackets first. Seven times two is fourteen.
Same numbers, different order, different answer. That is why the order matters.
