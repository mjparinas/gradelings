To add bigger numbers, we add the ones and the tens one at a time.
Break each number apart. Twenty-seven is two tens and seven ones. Thirty-six is three tens and six ones.
Add the ones first. Seven plus six makes thirteen.
Thirteen is one ten and three ones. Keep the three ones, and trade the ten ones for one more ten.
Now add the tens. Two tens, three tens and the new ten make six tens. Sixty plus three is sixty-three.
