Live questions let you ask the whole class one question and watch the answers arrive. First, the class timer.
Pick a length from one minute to thirty, add a note like Silent reading if you like, and start the timer. Students see it at the top of their screen. It never locks anything.
To ask a question, choose a unit under Ask from. Gradelings makes the question for you.
As students answer, the bars fill, and each desk turns green for right or red for wrong. Students who haven't answered yet stay grey.
Only you see the next question while the class works on this one. Choose a different question if you like. Then show the answer, or tap Next question.
Everything from live questions is deleted after a day, and it never changes anyone's levels. Look back at today's questions to see who to check in with.
