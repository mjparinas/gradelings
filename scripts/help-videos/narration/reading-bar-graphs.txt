A bar graph shows how many in each group. The taller the bar, the more there are.
Each bar has a label. Say these show the pets in a class. Fish, dogs and cats.
Look at the scale on the side. Each line is one more. We read the top of a bar against the scale.
The dogs bar is the tallest. So dogs is the most popular. The cats bar is the shortest.
To compare, subtract. Eight dogs and two cats. Eight minus two is six more dogs.
