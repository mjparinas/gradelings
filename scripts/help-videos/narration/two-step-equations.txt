An equation says two things are equal. We want to find the unknown number, x.
To solve it, undo the steps in reverse order. First, undo the plus three by subtracting three from both sides.
Next, undo the times two by dividing both sides by two.
Now check. Put four in place of x. Two times four is eight, and eight plus three is eleven. It works.
Whatever we do to one side, we do to the other side. That keeps the equation balanced.
