Perimeter is the distance around the outside of a shape.
Say a rectangle has sides of six and four. Opposite sides match, so we have two sixes and two fours.
Add all four sides. Six plus four plus six plus four.
So the perimeter is twenty units. Always add every side, and write the unit.
A shortcut. Add the two different sides, then double it. Six plus four is ten. Ten doubled is twenty.
