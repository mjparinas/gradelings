# Add a child before confirming the email (November 26, 2026)

Owner go-ahead after the conversion health check: 7 of 19 confirmed families in 90 days never added a child. Right after "Create account" in the parent area, the "Check your email" notice now has a "While you wait, add your child" card under it (`FirstChild` with `inline`, only when the device has no child). It takes a first name and a grade, and starts on the province or state and grade of the last region page the device read (`gradelings.region`, local only), like the step after confirming. The child is saved on this device and opens their first Adventure at once; it syncs when the confirmation link is opened on this device. Nothing changes on the server: sign-up still signs nobody in and answers everyone the same.
