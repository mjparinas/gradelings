# Parent how-to videos

**Parent how-to videos** (November 5, 2026, files in `public/videos/parents/`): short screen-recorded videos of the real parent area (demo child only), narrated with Supertonic, self-hosted with captions and the logo bottom-right, made by `scripts/help-videos/screens.py` (`docs/video-help/README.md`). They name no price or payment so they can show in the store apps. Each plays once on first use of its section (Overview, Children, Settings, Reports, Account; flag `training-videos`) through the shared player, and `#/videos` plus a footer link lists them all (`PARENT_VIDEOS` in `src/lib/trainingVideos.ts`).
