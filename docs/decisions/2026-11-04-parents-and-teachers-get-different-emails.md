# Parents and teachers get different emails

**Parents and teachers get different emails** (November 4, 2026): an account has a type (`parents.account_type`, `parent` or `teacher`, `src/server/audience.ts`), set when it is made from the teacher area, starts a class, accepts a co-teacher invitation or imports a roster. Emails choose their content by it. A teacher with no child of their own (`teacherOnly`) is skipped by the groups written for parents, the funnel, the to-do list's trial items and the daily job's trial recap and notice, practice reminders and weekly report (classes are free, so they have no trial to run out). The admin Emails tab has teacher groups (no class yet, no students yet, all teachers) and teacher presets, and Automations has two teacher nudges, both off until an admin types `TURN ON`. The Monday class email and the inactive-class warning were already teacher-only. A teacher who also adds a child still gets the parent emails. Account type decides email content only; it grants and removes nothing.
