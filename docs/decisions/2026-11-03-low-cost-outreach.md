# Low-cost outreach

**Low-cost outreach** (November 3, 2026): beyond Facebook, Instagram and Pinterest, growth comes from free tactics: helpful posts in Facebook groups and subreddits (always disclosing we make Gradelings), personal teacher outreach, creator codes, SEO pages, referrals and directory listings. Melvin approves every outreach message before it is sent or posted. Creators get a unique lifetime code (not `FRIENDS`) for an honest review and must disclose the free access. Drafts and lists live in `gradelings-social/outreach/`.
