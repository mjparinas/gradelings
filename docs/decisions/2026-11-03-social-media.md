# Social media

**Social media** (November 3, 2026): every post goes to Facebook, Instagram and Pinterest, each with its own sizes and caption. Post at the days and times with the most engagement, starting from benchmarks and re-ranked by link clicks from our own numbers after four weeks. Strategy, assets and records live in the `mjparinas/gradelings-social` repository, not on a desktop. Every post is recorded in that repo's posting log (platform, time, live link) with engagement numbers at 1, 7 and 30 days. Goals: organic visits to gradelings.com, sign-ups and upgrades, aimed at parents and teachers. Social copy says "Pre-K to Grade 9". No paid social and no tracking pixels without the owner's go-ahead; links carry UTM tags only. Free tools only (Meta Business Suite and Pinterest's own scheduler, no paid scheduler) and UTM links only, no pixels (owner, November 3, 2026). Claude drafts replies to comments and messages and Melvin sends them. Teachers and parents share the same accounts for now. Lifetime access codes may be offered to a few teacher and homeschool creators for honest reviews, with disclosure. **Graphics** (owner, November 3, 2026): the name Gradelings in any social graphic uses the website's per-letter colours (`WORDMARK_COLOURS` in `src/lib/brand.ts`), on a light panel so they show, and every graphic shows gradelings.com.
