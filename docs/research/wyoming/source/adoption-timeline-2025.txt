10        A Standards Review Committee convened and created the 2021 PE Standards. The State Board of Education (SBE) halted the work and did
          not approve the standards while they worked through an audit of the Standards Review Process. In 2022-23, the SBE requested educator
          committees to conduct an audit on the 2021 Math and Science Standards in order to set the state priorities and to create a more focused
          set of standards. In spring 2023, the SBE requested an educator committee perform a similar audit from the 2021 Standards that were not
          adopted.

11        A Standards Review Committee convened and created the 2021 Health Standards. Health Standards were developed with
          committee members, many of whom served on the P.E. committee following that work to ensure a cohesive approach to both
          sets of standards. The State Board of Education (SBE) halted the work and did the same as 12 above with an audit of the process
          and the standards. They also requested the committee to create a Suicide Prevention standard in the middle school and high
          school grade bands.
12        A Standards Review Committee convened and created the 2021 F&PA Standards. The State Board of Education (SBE) halted the
          work and did the same as 12 above with an audit of the process and the standards.
13        In 2018, HEA48 was enacted which required the addition of Computer Science (CS) Standards by January 1, 2022, to be effective
          beginning with the 2022-23 school year. The CS Content Standards were approved in early 2020 without the Performance
          Standards as the definition of PS was being refined. This is the first content area to have a subset of Performance Standards
          identified instead of the entire group of PLDs for the Proficient Performance Levels. The CS Performance Standards were
          approved April 7, 2021.
