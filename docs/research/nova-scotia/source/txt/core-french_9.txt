Outcomes – Core French 9 (2024)


 GCO 1 : Based on his/her experiences in the Core French program, the student should be able
   to communicate effectively in French and be able to interact appropriately in a variety of
                      situations relative to his/her needs and interests.

 1.2 follow directions that are increasingly complex
 1.4 ask for, give information

 1.10 give directions
 1.11 discuss own tastes
 1.13 justify own choices
 1.19 choose pertinent information
 1.21 identify the main ideas of a text
 1.27 produce a variety of texts (personal, narratiave and informational)
 1.28 revise and correct own text
 1.41 discuss elements of Acadian and other francophone cultures.
