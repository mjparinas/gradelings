# Prince Edward Island curriculum: source and checking record

- **Source:** the Prince Edward Island Department of Education and Lifelong Learning (princeedwardisland.ca, Programs of Study). The department's site blocks automated downloads, so a person saved the PDFs (see `fetch.sh`) and they are kept in `source/`:
  - `eelc_outcomes_framework_{k,1,2,3,4,5,6}.pdf`: the *Specific Curriculum Outcomes Framework* for Kindergarten and Grades 1 to 6 (2013): every specific curriculum outcome, by code, in English language arts, mathematics, science, social studies and (Grades 4 to 6) Core French.
  - `eelc_math_9_5.pdf` and `eelc_socialstudies_9.pdf`: the Grade 9 mathematics guide and the Grade 9 social studies guide (*Global Connections*, 2025).
  - `intermediate_program_of_studies_and_authorized_materials.pdf` (2022-2023) and `eelc_program_of_studies_k-6.pdf`: course descriptions, course codes (for example 7SOCA) and time allotments; `icdr_website_doc_2025.pdf`: the department's notice on intermediate curriculum renewal, which names the CAMET competencies.
- **Outcomes:** `outcomes.json` holds the outcomes read from those PDFs (`{"<grade>/<subject>": [{"code", "group", "text"}]}`, 882 entries). `EXTRACTION_REPORT.md` records how, the checks (0 failures across 874 PDF-sourced entries), the counts and the typos in the PDFs, which are kept as printed. Rebuild and verify with `extract_frameworks.py`, `build_outcomes.py` and `verify_outcomes.py`; `build_overall.py` writes the course overviews in `src/content/prince-edward-island/overall.ts`.
- **How units cite them:** by outcome code, joined with "; " (for example `N1; N3` in math, `4.3` in English language arts, `4.2.1` in social studies). Science cites the unit title ("Life Science: Habitats"). `prince-edward-island.test.ts` checks every citation against `outcomes.json`.
- **Plan and units:** `src/content/prince-edward-island/plan.ts` lists, for each grade and subject, the BC units shared and the units copied from other provinces (only where their questions name no other province). Units written for Prince Edward Island (`pe-` ids, in `src/content/prince-edward-island/units/`) fill the gaps, mostly in science and social studies, which are specific to the province (for example Grade 4 Exploration, Grade 5 societies of long ago and the Middle Ages, Grade 6 Prince Edward Island history and world cultures).
- **Provincial assessments:** the programs of study name the Primary and Elementary Literacy Assessments and the Primary and Elementary Mathematics Assessments, held at the end of Grade 3 and Grade 6 (search results from the department's pages; the schedule changes, so the guide points to the department).
- **French:** Core French begins no later than Grade 4, and Grades 1 to 3 are handled locally on a pilot basis (program of studies); the framework prints Core French outcomes for Grades 4 to 6. Early (from Kindergarten), Middle (Grade 4) and Late (Grade 7) French Immersion exist, but their curriculum guides are not in the repository, so French Immersion is not built.

## Known gaps
- **Grades 7 and 8 have no outcomes.** The Grade 7 and 8 guides are not in the repository. Math cites the four strands, English language arts the ten general curriculum outcomes, science the units named in the program of studies, social studies the course themes, and Core French a single placeholder. Replace these when the guides are added. Grade 9 math and social studies use the Grade 9 guides; Grade 9 English language arts, science and Core French also use placeholders.
- **Report card:** a Prince Edward Island report-card scale could not be confirmed (both the department's and the Public Schools Branch's sites block downloads), so the framework uses the generic practice steps (Beginning, Approaching, Meeting, Exceeding).
- **Edition:** the Kindergarten to Grade 6 frameworks date from 2013 and the Intermediate Program of Studies from 2022-2023; check them against the current editions (the department publishes a 2026-2027 Intermediate Program of Studies).
- **Outcomes with no unit:** the partial gaps in math, English language arts and Core French (listed in `src/content/prince-edward-island/plan.ts`'s source plans) were not written as units; only science and social studies gaps were. A teacher should review which outcomes still need practice units.
- **Grades 1 and 2 English language arts** repeat codes across stage bands (Emergent, Early, Transitional), so a citation such as `1.1` can refer to either band. Grade 6 social studies repeats unit numbers across its two organizers (PEI History, World Cultures).
- **Prince Edward Island units** were written by the project with partial fact-checking. Several were written from general knowledge without a web check (Grade 2 and Grade 9 in particular); a teacher should review all of them.
