Statewide Assessments

Vermont Comprehensive Assessment System 

The Agency of Education coordinates the implementation and administration of all components of the Vermont Comprehensive Assessment System (VTCAS), including the administration of alternate assessments. The Agency also identifies, analyzes, and reports on outcomes and data measured by the VTCAS. Established by the State Board of Education in 1996, the VTCAS evaluates student performance in the state's schools based on Common Core State Standards (CCSS), Next Generation Science Standards, and English Language Development Standards with the goal of improving teaching and learning.   

Statewide assessments can provide useful information for comparing student achievement:  

  • in the same subject and grade level across school years;  

  • between student groups; or 

  • in comparison to statewide benchmarks. 

Statewide assessments should not be used to compare student achievement for any consequential purpose:  

  • across different subjects and different grade levels; or  

  • across testing groups with too few students to provide for valid inferences (Hollingshead and Childs, 2011). 

Schools use a wide variety of assessments, but for the most part, locally developed assessments do not have the reliability and technical qualities necessary to support valid inferences about the progress and performance of schools, and about specific groups of students within those schools. Statewide assessments will help us assess the success of our efforts to close performance gap.

Assessment of English Language Proficiency 

All K-12 students identified as English learners, including those with the most significant cognitive disabilities, take the State’s annual English language proficiency assessment to monitor their progress in acquiring academic English. 

Vermont participates in the WIDA consortium of states working together to meet the requirements for standards and assessments outlined in the Every Student Succeeds Act (ESSA). The WIDA ACCESS, ACCESS for Kindergarten, and Alternate ACCESS assessments are aligned with WIDA English Language Development Standards and measure proficiency in each of four domains: reading, writing, listening, and speaking. 

For more information visit the Vermont WIDA site . 

Assessment of Academic Proficiency 

All students in grades 3-9 are required to take the State’s annual academic assessment in mathematics and English language arts, and all students in grades 5, 8, and 11 are required to take the State’s annual academic assessment in science. 

Most students will fulfill this requirement by taking the Vermont Comprehensive Assessment Program (VTCAP). Students on IEPs with the most significant disabilities may fulfill this requirement by taking the Multi-State Alternate Assessment (MSAA). The VTCAP and the MSAA collectively fulfill the State’s federal accountability requirements under ESSA regarding the assessment of academic proficiency in math, science, and English language arts. 

For more information about VTCAP, visit the Vermont Help & Support site. For more information about MSAA, visit the Alternate Assessment site. 

Test Administration Windows 

School Year 2025-2026 

The WIDA ACCESS, ACCESS for Kindergarten, and Alternate ACCESS will be administered from January 5 to March 13, 2026. 

The National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP) in Reading and Mathematics will be administered from January 26 to March 20, 2026.

The VTCAP and the MSAA will be administered from March 9 to April 24, 2026. A make-up window for the VTCAP only will be from April 27 to May 1. 

Results and Reporting 

Accessing Results 

School and district level assessment results are publicly available on the Vermont Education Dashboard. State assessment results are used in Vermont’s school accountability system and related results can be found on the Vermont Annual Snapshot.  

If you work for a school or district, contact your School or District Test Coordinator for access to secure student-level results. For more information on the use of secure results see Use of Assessment Data in the Assessment Management Systems.

Statewide Assessment Team 

The Statewide Assessment Team provides oversight, support and leadership of the administration and reporting of state and national assessments in Vermont. Contact the team at AOE.StatewideAssessment@vermont.gov.
