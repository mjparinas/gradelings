Overview


Personal Finance focuses on the fundamentals of personal finance, income, spending, saving and
investing, risk and insurance, and credit, with a goal to develop individuals who can manage their
personal finances. Personal finance is also known as personal financial literacy and financial
literacy. The standards are written as grade-banded standards built around the areas below and
topics that make up the areas of study.

     Number                              Areas

       17.1         Personal Finance Fundamentals
       17.2         Income
       17.3         Spending
       17.4         Saving & Investing
       17.5         Risk & Insurance
       17.6         Credit
