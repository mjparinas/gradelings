Pennsylvania social studies, Kindergarten to Grade 4: standards with no unit, and why.

Every "standard" entry for social studies in Kindergarten to Grade 4 in outcomes.json is cited by at least one unit in that grade's course
(src/content/pennsylvania/plan/social-k4.ts for borrowed units, units/social-{k,g1,g2,g3,g4}.ts for Pennsylvania's own). Nothing is left out
for lack of a unit. What follows is where the practice is thinner than the standard asks, and the standards PDE repeats at every grade.

Practised only by recognition (multiple choice), because the standard asks for more than a tap can show:
5.2.*.B / 5.3.*.F | participating in the community, public service and solving problems: the units ask what a citizen or a fair plan is, not whether a child does it
8.1.*.B / 8.1.4.B | gathering and comparing sources: the units ask which source is primary or secondary and which statement is fact or opinion, not to research one
8.3.*.C / 8.3.*.D | comparing two accounts of an event and continuity and change: practised through short items and the timeline, not through writing about them
7.3.*.A / 7.4.*.B | human characteristics of places and how people change the land: practised with named Pennsylvania examples (cities, mining, floods), not with a child's own place
17.3.*.M | fundraising and giving: practised as what and why, not as a plan

Standards that repeat across a band (K-2, 3-5): each grade cites the ones its units practise, and a few are cited in one grade of the band
and not in its neighbour (for example 17.5.3-5.A to C, insurance and emergency savings, are practised in Grades 3 and 4; opportunity cost, 17.1.3-5.B, in Grades 3 and 4).
Grade 5 is another worker's, so these were not left to it.

Native peoples (FACTS.md section 6): Lenape, Susquehannock, Shawnee, Seneca and other nations are named as living peoples in the present tense, with the places their
communities are today (Oklahoma, Wisconsin, New York). No federally recognized tribe has its seat in Pennsylvania. The content is light on purpose, has nothing sacred or
ceremonial, and needs partner review before it grows. The Walking Purchase is told as an unfair land deal, not as a legend.

World history (8.4.4.A to D, Grade 4): one unit (pa-g4-world-history) of famous sites, documents, groups, change and continuity, and cooperation and conflict. It is a survey,
not a course; PDE's wording is broad, so many world-history events have no item.

Borrowed units that were tried and dropped because their questions name Canada, another province or another state: helpers-and-rules, different-and-alike, then-and-now-1,
mb-g1-needs-wants, mb-g1-getting-along, mb-natural-resources, mb-what-makes-a-community, mb-maps-and-directions, mb-leaders-and-rights, maps-and-globes, world-cultures,
ar-g3-money-choices (an Arkansas own unit that Arkansas's contract does not share), al-g2-moving, al-g2-us-regions, al-g2-american-symbols, or-g2-world-maps,
wa-g2-reading-maps, ca-g1-american-symbols.
