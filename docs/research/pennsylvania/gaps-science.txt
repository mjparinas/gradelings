Pennsylvania science: STEELS standards no unit practises yet (October 2026)

Each Pennsylvania science course cites the STEELS standards in docs/research/pennsylvania/outcomes.json. The 3.1 life science, 3.2 physical science and 3.3 Earth and space science standards are all practised (the 6-8 band across Grades 6, 7 and 8, which are placed by topic: Earth and space in Grade 6, life science and the environment in Grade 7, physical science and engineering in Grade 8). The 3.4 environment and 3.5 technology and engineering standards are practised where a multiple-choice or tap question can check them; the ones below are not, and why.

Why a standard has no unit:
- It asks the student to make, build, repair, program, plan or manage something with tools and materials (3.5 'make', 'diagnose and repair', 'project management', 'quality control'): practice here cannot check the doing.
- It asks for research, a written explanation, a defended argument, a presentation or a portfolio in the student's own words: the app takes no free text.
- It is about the school's or a class's own project, community or workplace (careers, business, marketplaces), which the app has no way to see.
- It repeats a standard that a unit already practises in other words.
