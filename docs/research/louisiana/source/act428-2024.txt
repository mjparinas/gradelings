 1                                             AN ACT

 2   To enact R.S. 17:184 and 3996(B)(82) and to repeal R.S. 17:7(31), relative to grades

 3          assigned in public schools; to require all public schools to use a uniform ten-point

 4          grading scale; to repeal the requirement that the State Board of Elementary and

 5          Secondary Education provide for the implementation of a uniform grading scale; and

 6          to provide for related matters.

 7   Be it enacted by the Legislature of Louisiana:

 8          Section 1. R.S. 17:184 and 3996(B)(82) are hereby enacted to read as follows:

 9          §184. Uniform grading scale

10                  A. Each public school governing authority shall use the following ten-point

11          grading scale for students enrolled in any grade for which letter grades are used:

12                  (1) One hundred points through ninety points is an A.

13                  (2) Eighty-nine points through eighty points is a B.

14                  (3) Seventy-nine points through seventy points is a C.

15                  (4) Sixty-nine points through sixty points is a D.

16                  (5) Fifty-nine points through zero points in an F.

17                  B. The provisions of Subsection A of this Section do not apply to any school

18          that is operated by the United States Department of Defense and that is located on

19          a federal military installation.
