# Newfoundland and Labrador curriculum: source and checking record

- **Source:** the Newfoundland and Labrador Department of Education and Early Childhood Development, gov.nl.ca/education/k12/curriculum/. The English program follows the Atlantic Canada curricula. The only curriculum documents the department publishes openly are the **foundation documents**: *Foundation for the Atlantic Canada English Language Arts Curriculum*, *Foundations for the Atlantic Canada Mathematics Curriculum*, *Foundation for the Atlantic Canada Science Curriculum*, *Foundation for the Atlantic Canada Social Studies Curriculum* (all under "Foundation/Framework Documents") and the *Second Language Core French Curriculum* orientation document (under "Core French – Curriculum Support"). Each states **general curriculum outcomes** and **key-stage curriculum outcomes**: what students are expected to achieve by the end of Grade 3, Grade 6, Grade 9 and Grade 12.
- **Rebuild:** `sh fetch.sh <work-dir>` downloads the five PDFs and runs `pdftotext` (layout and plain). `python3 -I build_outcomes.py <work-dir> ../../../src/content/newfoundland-and-labrador/overall.ts > outcomes.json` writes `outcomes.json` and the course overviews in `overall.ts`. `extract_stages.py` reads the two-column key-stage tables. The build fails if any outcome is not found word for word in the plain text of its document.
- **How units cite them:** by strand and key stage, for example "Operation sense (by Grade 6)". Kindergarten to Grade 3 cite the Grade 3 stage, Grades 4 to 6 the Grade 6 stage, and Grades 7 to 9 the Grade 9 stage. A unit can cite several, separated by "; ". `content.test.ts` and `newfoundland-and-labrador.test.ts` check every citation against `outcomes.json` and that its stage matches the grade. Each `outcomes.json` row is `idx` (the citation), the strand, the stage, the general curriculum outcome (`gco`) and the key-stage outcomes for that stage (the outcomes added at that stage; a stage also includes those of the earlier stage).
- **Strands:** mathematics has seven (number sense and number theory, operation sense, patterns and relationships, measurement, spatial sense, data management, probability); English language arts has ten, grouped under speaking and listening, reading and viewing, and writing and representing; science has four (science, technology, society and the environment, skills of inquiry, knowledge, attitudes), and outcomes there carry official numbers such as (302); social studies has six (citizenship, power and governance; culture and diversity; individuals, societies and economic decisions; interdependence; people, place and environment; time, continuity and change); Core French has four (communication, culture, general language education, language). In science, the Grade 3 stage combines knowledge with the first strand, so Kindergarten to Grade 3 science cites "Science, technology, society and the environment".
- **Report card:** Kindergarten to Grade 6 use the four-point rating of the K-6 report card (4 Excellent, 3 Good, 2 Approaching, 1 Not Yet Achieving, plus IE and Not Applicable), from the Newfoundland and Labrador English School District's *K-6 Report Card Support Document* (nlschools.ca). Grades 7 to 9 report a percentage mark for each course (policy PROG-317, *Assessment, Evaluation and Reporting*, 5.14: marks of 46 to 49% are not reported). The department's named bands for Grades 7 to 9 could not be confirmed, so those grades use the generic practice steps (Beginning, Approaching, Meeting, Exceeding).
- **Provincial assessments:** the department lists the Provincial Reading and Mathematics Assessments (PRMA) for Grades 3 and 6 (gov.nl.ca/education/k12/prma/). Check the department's site for the schedule before launch.
- **French:** Core French is organized from Grade 4 and most students in Grades 4 to 9 take it (Atlantic Core French orientation document, "Core French Curriculum in the Atlantic Provinces"). Early French Immersion runs from Kindergarten and Late French Immersion from Grade 7 (gov.nl.ca/education/k12/french/immersion/description/).

## Known gaps
- **The grade-by-grade curriculum guides are not public.** They moved to the department's eNLightenED site, which requires a login (an unauthenticated request returns 401), and the older PDFs on gov.nl.ca return 404. So the units cite key-stage outcomes, not each grade's own specific outcomes. **Where a unit sits within a key stage is provisional**: math and language use the units Nova Scotia and New Brunswick both teach in that grade (all three follow the Atlantic Canada and Western and Northern Canadian Protocol outcomes), science uses the units either neighbour teaches in that grade, and social studies uses general units chosen by key stage plus the Newfoundland and Labrador-only units. Replace the citations and the grade placement when the guides can be read.
- **French Immersion is not built** (no Kindergarten to Grade 9 Immersion for Newfoundland and Labrador), because its curriculum guides are only on eNLightenED.
- **Science and social studies topics by grade** (for example which Grade 4 science units NL teaches) could not be confirmed.
- **Prince Edward Island is not built.** The Prince Edward Island department's site (princeedwardisland.ca) sits behind a bot check that blocks automated downloads, so its curriculum documents could not be read.
- **Newfoundland and Labrador units** (`nl-` ids, written for this province): Our Province (Grade 3), Peoples of Newfoundland and Labrador (Grade 4), Early Visitors and The Fishery and Outport Life (Grade 5), How We Are Governed (Grade 6), Resources and Choices (Grade 7), Newfoundland Joins Canada (Grade 8) and Land Claims and Self-Government in Labrador (Grade 9). Their sources are listed in the top comment of each file in `src/content/newfoundland-and-labrador/units/`.
- **Borrowed units:** units from other provinces were dropped where their questions named another province (checked by `content.test.ts`).
