Child’s Learning Journey

RISE assessments are one way to understand your
child’s learning journey. They show how well your child is meeting
Utah’s Core Standards and highlight the growth they’re making from year to year.

Proficiency Levels
Proficiency levels are a simple way to understand test results. They show whether your child is
meeting grade-level expectations and highlight areas where they may need extra support or are
excelling.

        Below Proficient
  1     Student does not yet show an understanding of grade-level standards. They partly work
        with grade-level material and need a lot of support for higher-level thinking.


        Approaching Proficient
  2     Student shows a partial understanding and is close to meeting grade-level standards.
        They demonstrate some higher-level thinking but still need support.


        Proficient
  3     Student shows a solid understanding of grade-level standards. They handle grade-level
        material and use some higher-level thinking with little support.

        Highly Proficient
  4     Student shows an advanced understanding of grade-level standards. They work with
        grade-level material on their own and use higher-level thinking skills.

Understanding Your Child’s Individual Student Report (ISR)
After testing, families receive an ISR. This is the main document that explains your child’s
Summative RISE results. RISE Summative Assessments are required end-of-course assessments
and are computer adaptive, meaning the difficulty of questions adjusts to each student’s
performance. These results usually arrive within three weeks of your school’s testing window.
The report highlights:
•    Proficiency Levels: How well your child met grade-level expectations.
•    Scale Score: A number that shows an overall performance score, adjusted for the difficulty of
     the questions.
•    Score Comparisons: Student’s score compared to averages for their school, district, and the
     state.
•    Reporting Categories: Breaks results into smaller skill areas, like reading or math topics.
•    Year-to-Year Trends: Shows growth over multiple years, report available from the school.
