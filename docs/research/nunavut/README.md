# Nunavut research record

**Sources:** Nunavut Department of Education, "Nunavut Approved Curriculum and Teaching Resources" (August 2019, gov.nu.ca, 120 pages) and its 2023-24 edition (ilinniapaa-learning.ca copy, August 2023), which has the same structure; "Curriculum and Teaching Resources Orientation 2023/2024" (gov.nu.ca); the Department's news on the new Nunavut curriculum (Kindergarten and Grade 1 from 2025-26). The Department's web pages returned 403 to automated reads, so the PDFs are the checked source.

## What the sources say
- Teachers must use the approved curriculum guides. They are "produced in Nunavut" or "adopted from other jurisdictions including NWT, Alberta, Saskatchewan, and Manitoba".
- Four strands: Nunavusiutit (heritage and culture, history, geography, environmental science, civics and economics), Iqqaqqaukkaringniq (mathematics, analytical and critical thinking, solution-seeking), Uqausiliriniq (communication and language) and Aulajaaqtut (wellness and safety).
- Mathematics K-9: the Alberta K-9 Mathematics Program of Studies (2007, updated 2014). Science K-6: NWT Science and Technology K-6 (2004). Science 7-9: Nunavut Adaptation of the Alberta Science 7-8-9 program (2003, adapted 2014). English language arts: NWT K-3, 4-7 and 7-9 curricula (2011). Social studies: Inuuqatigiit (NWT, 1996), NWT Elementary Social Studies Grades 1-6 (1993), Junior Secondary Social Studies (NWT, 1993), Civics in the Junior Secondary Social Studies Curriculum (NWT, 1998), the Nunavut Land Claims Agreement, and a Nunavut Grade 9 Social Studies teacher's guide (2015). French: French as an Additional Language for Nunavut, Grades 4-9 (Manitoba, 2014).
- Inuktitut Language Arts K-6 (Nunavut, 1999) and the Inuktut Titiqqiriniq literacy program are Nunavut's own; they are not built here.
- The Department has identified core outcomes for each subject (Kindergarten to Grade 9) and core curriculum checklists.
- A new Nunavut curriculum is being phased in, starting with Kindergarten and Grade 1 in 2025-26, and is complete for Kindergarten to Grade 6 on paper. See "The new Nunavut curriculum" below.

## The new Nunavut curriculum (checked 12 October 2026)
- **Sources:** Government of Nunavut news release "Government of Nunavut announces Nunavut curriculum launch" (13 March 2025; repost at educationnewscanada.com): grounded in Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit, teaching materials in Inuktut and English, Kindergarten and Grade 1 in every community in 2025-26, Kindergarten to Grade 6 complete, one new grade a year until Kindergarten to Grade 12 in 2036. The Department's curriculum page (gov.nu.ca, quoted in search results): Kindergarten and Grade 1 in fall 2025, Grades 2 and 3 in fall 2026, then one more grade each year. Nunavut News (26 August 2026): 2026-27 is "the first time Grade 2 and 3 students will be taught" it, and Grades 4 to 12 still use the Alberta-based curriculum. APTN's version of that story names Grade 2 only; this app follows the Department.
- **Documents:** the Approved Curriculum and Curriculum Resources pages list a Kindergarten to Grade 6 set (Mathematics; English Language Arts, 1st Language; Science; Social Studies; plus Inuktut, English 2nd Language, Physical Education, Health and Wellness, and Arts Education) with orientation guides for educators. gov.nu.ca answers automated reads with a Cloudflare check (HTTP 403), so the owner downloaded the documents and supplied them in October 2026. They are in `new-curriculum/` with the outcomes extracted to `new-curriculum/outcomes.json` (see its README).
- **How Kindergarten to Grade 3 cite it:** the subject document and the outcome codes the unit practises: "Nunavut Mathematics K–6 (2025), K.N.1.1; K.N.1.4", "Nunavut Science K–6 (2025), 2.LS.1.1". In `approved.json` those grades carry `inForceFrom`, the guides they `replace` and `areas: null`; `nunavut.test.ts` checks every code against `new-curriculum/outcomes.json` for the unit's grade and subject.
- **Units (checked against the documents, October 2026):** each Kindergarten to Grade 3 unit was matched to the outcomes, units with no matching outcome were dropped (general "be a scientist" skills units, structures and speed in Grade 3 science, Canada and celebrations in Kindergarten, world neighbours in Grade 1) and units were added where an outcome had none: coins and money in every grade, adding and taking away and the days of the week in Kindergarten, numbers to 100, halves, the calendar, position words and graphs in Grade 1, numbers to 1000, quarters, equal groups and symmetry in Grade 2, slides in Grade 3; push and pull and weather in Kindergarten, structures and forces in Grade 1, changing materials, light and sound and life cycles in Grade 2, particles, thermal energy, landforms, erosion, rocks and food chains in Grade 3; my story, then and now, communities in Canada, leaders and lands and waters in social studies. A "lift" entry copies a unit from another grade where the new curriculum moved the topic: Grade 3 Numbers to 10 000 (BC Grade 4) and Rocks vs. Minerals (Prince Edward Island Grade 4), Grade 1 Push & Pull (BC Kindergarten).
- **Partly covered:** Grade 3 decimal point (3.N.1.8), capacity and temperature (3.SS.1.6, 3.SS.1.10), Grade 3 light and sound energy (3.P.1.6, 3.P.1.7) and the rock cycle (3.ES.1.3), Grade 3 matter having mass (3.MW.1.1 to 3.MW.1.5), Grade 2 how air, water and animals move over the land (2.L.1, 2.L.3) and caring for it beyond Caring Citizens, sorting and data questions in Kindergarten and Grade 1 math. Outcomes on traditional stories and knowledge (for example K.ES.1.6, 1.L.4, 2.LS.1.6) and Inuit oral narrative are not practised: they belong to Elders and the classroom.
- **Grade 3 social studies** is about Indigenous Peoples in Canada and the world (3.C.1 to 3.C.8, 3.L.4, 3.L.5). Units about other Indigenous Peoples are never borrowed for Nunavut, so this grade practises only maps, leadership, comparing ways of life and inquiry, plus Our Territory. It needs Nunavut-written units with partner review.
- **Every September:** move the next grade (Grade 4 in 2027-28) the same way: `approved.json`, `plan.ts`, any own units of that grade, and `NEW_CURRICULUM_GRADES` in `src/content/nunavut/overall.ts`.

## Mapping
- `approved.json` lists, per grade and subject, the approved guides and the strand names a unit may cite. `nunavut.test.ts` checks every Nunavut citation against it.
- `src/content/nunavut/plan.ts` was generated once from the Manitoba pool (Kindergarten to Grade 6 math and science, all language, social studies and Core French) and the Alberta pool (Grades 7-9 math and science). BC units are shared; the rest are copied with Nunavut standards only. Units whose questions name another province, city or Indigenous nation were left out.
- Social studies: only skills, citizenship, community and map units were reused. World-history units (ancient, medieval) were left out because the approved NWT guides are not those courses.
- Skills units written for Nunavut (`src/content/nunavut/units/skills.ts`): compass directions between real communities (Grade 4, rounded coordinates, asked only when clearly one compass point), map scale (Grade 4), time zones (Grade 8, only communities whose zone is settled: Qikiqtaaluk Eastern, Kivalliq Central, Kitikmeot Mountain; Coral Harbour keeps its own time and is left out) and population data with made-up communities (Grade 9). Recheck community time zones before launch.

## Known gaps
- Citations are by guide and strand, not outcome code, because the NWT guides and the 2007 Alberta program text could not be read automatically. The match for Kindergarten to Grade 6 math and for language is by topic (WNCP-based Manitoba units), not outcome by outcome. Check against the guides before launch.
- No territory-wide report-card scale or test schedule could be confirmed, so the framework uses four plain practice steps and the assessment guide says to ask the school.
- Nunavut-only units contain Inuit and Inuktut content (Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit principles, the land claim, land skills, a few Inuktitut words). Facts come from public Government of Nunavut and NTI material but the content is light. Dates and names (signing dates, boards) should be rechecked.
- Inuktut Language Arts, the Inuktut Titiqqiriniq program, Inuinnaqtun and Aulajaaqtut (wellness) are not built. Partial gaps in the new curriculum for Kindergarten to Grade 3 are listed above.
- The Grade 10 to 12 courses are out of scope.
