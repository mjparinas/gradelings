Paper/Pencil Testing
 • Schools assessing students with paper/pencil
   assessments must select an initial test date for
   each grade and content area and assess as
   many students as possible on the initial dates.
   Test parts should be scheduled early enough
   in the school day to allow students to complete
   the entire test within the school day. ELA Day 1
   should be scheduled for a single day and ELA
   Day 2 should be scheduled on a different day.
 • Estimated paper/pencil test session times do
   not include the following:
    » traveling to and from the testing room
    » distributing and collecting test materials,
      including test booklets and answer documents
    » completing the answer document
      demographic page
    » listening to test directions and reviewing
      sample items
