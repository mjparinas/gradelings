# How often each province and territory updates its K–9 curriculum

Researched 12 October 2026 from ministry pages, government news releases and the repo's own research notes (`docs/research/<province>/README.md`). Where a source was secondary (news) or a point is my inference, it says so.

## The short version

- **No province publishes a fixed calendar** ("every N years") for K–9. Manitoba's Framework for Learning describes a "systematic, cyclical" renewal process but gives no interval. In practice, a subject is rewritten roughly every **10 to 20 years**, and provinces do it in waves of one subject or grade band at a time. *(The 10–20 year figure is my inference from the edition dates below.)*
- **Change is driven by politics and test scores more than by a schedule.** Ontario, Alberta, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick and Nunavut are all currently in "back to basics" literacy and numeracy rewrites.
- **The three territories borrow:** Yukon and the NWT follow BC (adapted), and Nunavut is moving from adopted Alberta and NWT guides to its own curriculum, one grade a year.

## Province by province

### British Columbia
- **Last major rewrite:** the "redesigned curriculum" (Big Ideas, Curricular Competencies, Content), K–9 mandatory from September 2016 after a 2015 transition. Since then there have only been small edits, such as Indigenous content and the 2023–24 graduation changes for Grades 10–12.
- **Cycle:** none published. No K–9 revision is announced as of October 2026. (I found nothing on curriculum.gov.bc.ca or in BC news releases; the March 2026 Fraser Institute study criticizes the redesign but announces nothing.)
- Sources: curriculum.gov.bc.ca; [CBC, 2016 rollout](https://amp.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/new-flexible-school-curriculum-goes-province-wide-next-week-1.3743299); [Fraser Institute](https://www.fraserinstitute.org/studies/analyzing-british-columbias-education-curriculum-how-has-it-changed).

### Ontario
- **Editions in force:** Math 1–8 (2020), Grade 9 math MTH1W (2021), Science and Technology 1–8 (2022), Grade 9 science SNC1W (2022), Language 1–8 (2023), Social Studies, History and Geography (2018, updated 2023), FSL (2013), Kindergarten (2026, from September 2026).
- **Cycle:** in practice one subject at a time, every 10–15 years. The pace sped up since 2020. In June 2025 the province paused changes and moved the Kindergarten rollout to 2026–27. In December 2025 the minister appointed an expert advisory body to review student learning after EQAO results (secondary source).
- **What's new from September 2026:** more learning on Black history in Canada, the Holocaust and the Holodomor (grades not specified).
- Sources: [ontario.ca "Getting back to basics"](https://www.ontario.ca/page/preparing-students-future) (updated September 11, 2026); [CTV, June 2025 pause](https://www.ctvnews.ca/toronto/article/ontario-announces-pause-on-school-curriculum-changes-including-kindergarten-overhaul/); dcp.edu.gov.on.ca.

### Alberta
- **Rolling renewal, 2022–2027:** K–3 math and ELA (September 2022); Grades 4–6 math and ELA, K–3 science and French (2023–24); Grades 4–6 science and French (2024–25); K–3 social studies (September 2025); Grades 4–6 social studies (September 2026).
- **Next:** Grades 7–9 math, social studies, career education and financial literacy, and wellness. Drafts were released in 2025–26, there is an optional pilot in 2026–27, and they become mandatory in **September 2027**. Draft Grades 7–9 science is in optional field testing in 2026–27, with no implementation date yet. Grades 7–9 ELA and French have no date.
- Source: [alberta.ca, Subject areas (renewal timeline)](https://www.alberta.ca/curriculum-subject-areas).

### Saskatchewan
- **Last major rewrite:** the "renewed curricula" of roughly 2008–2011 (outcomes and indicators). Since then the updates have been targeted.
- **Now:** draft Kindergarten oral-language and writing scope and sequence documents (2026), a final reading scope and sequence, a preliminary First Nations and Métis Language Framework 1–9 (2026), and secondary ELA renewal (ELA 20 and 30, 2025 preliminary). Provincial assessments are being phased in from 2025–26 (math in Grades 5 and 9, ELA in Grades 4, 7 and 10).
- **Cycle:** none published.
- Sources: [curriculum.gov.sk.ca](https://curriculum.gov.sk.ca/CurriculumHome?id=405); [assessment program](https://educationnewscanada.com/article/education/level/k12/3/1114095/work-begins-on-new-saskatchewan-student-assessment-program.html).

### Manitoba
- **Framework for Learning** (2023 onward) sets up a "systematic, cyclical curriculum renewal process", starting with physical and health education and science. No interval is given.
- **Science K–10:** renewed. Piloted from 2024, voluntary in 2025–26, full implementation in **2026–27**. Gradelings already cites it.
- **Math:** K–8 is still the 2013 framework. Two new compulsory Grade 9 courses with financial literacy are in pilot or voluntary use in 2026–27 and become full in **September 2027**.
- **ELA and social studies:** no renewal date found.
- Sources: [Framework for Learning](https://www.edu.gov.mb.ca/k12/framework/index.html); [CBC, Grade 9 math](https://amp.cbc.ca/news/canada/manitoba/financial-literacy-manitoba-math-curriculum-1.7537221); [CTV, science rollout](https://www.ctvnews.ca/winnipeg/article/new-science-curriculum-draws-concerns-from-teachers-across-the-province/).

### Nova Scotia
- **Recent:** a redesigned Primary–3 math and literacy curriculum came with a new P–3 report card. ELA was rewritten for Primary–2 (2024 documents) and for Grades 3–6 (2025 documents, in classrooms September 2026). Grades 7–9 ELA is from 2022. Math still follows the WNCP-based guides from around 2019.
- **Cycle:** none published. The department's curriculum site says curricula are "being renewed" with a literacy and numeracy focus.
- Sources: [curriculum.novascotia.ca](https://curriculum.novascotia.ca/); [Sept 2026 ELA rollout](https://educationnewscanada.com/article/education/region/nova-scotia/34/1215940/students-return-to-class-with-expanded-lunch-program-new-literacy-curriculum.html); [Action Plan](https://www.ednet.ns.ca/educationreview/).

### New Brunswick (anglophone)
- **Recent:** the K–5 Holistic Mathematics Curriculum has been in all anglophone schools since fall 2025. The documents ("v0.3 August 2026") are on a staff-only SharePoint. A new 12-year anglophone education plan, *Strong Basics, Bright Futures* (June 2026), runs in three-year action cycles focused on literacy and numeracy, and started in September 2026.
- **Cycle:** the plan's three-year action cycles concern goals, not curriculum documents.
- Sources: [district letter on K–5 math](https://keswickridge.nbed.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/41/2025/10/Letter-to-Familes-Regarding-Mathematics-2025-26.pdf); [GNB, September 2026](https://www.gnb.ca/en/news/n-b.2026.09.new-education-plans-being-implemented-start-school-year.html); curriculum.nbed.ca.

### Newfoundland and Labrador
- **Recent:** all K–12 curriculum moved to the eNLightenED portal (2024–25), which the department says allows "more agile" renewal. A new Grades 7–9 program started in September 2025. Subject updates in 2024 included Social Studies 1–2, Core French 5, Immersion K–2 and Français 9. A K–6 Responsive Teaching and Learning framework was introduced earlier.
- **Cycle:** none stated. The portal needs a login, so we can't see grade-by-grade changes.
- Source: [gov.nl.ca, August 2024](https://www.gov.nl.ca/releases/2024/education/0823n01/).

### Prince Edward Island
- **Recent:** intermediate (7–9) restructuring from September 2023 (30% language arts, 60-minute blocks, wider exploratory courses). The K–6 Specific Curriculum Outcomes Framework is from 2013. The Grade 9 social studies guide is from 2025.
- **Cycle:** none published.
- Sources: [PEI intermediate notice](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/eb65/icdr_website_doc_2025.pdf); `docs/research/prince-edward-island/README.md`.

### Yukon
- Follows the BC curriculum adapted for the Yukon, so it changes when BC does. No separate cycle.
- Source: yukon.ca/en/school-curriculum.

### Northwest Territories
- Replacing Alberta with BC adapted for the NWT, in divisions from 2023: Grades 4–6 final in 2025–26, Grades 7–9 final in 2026–27, Kindergarten to Grade 3 still in draft. Dates have moved before.
- Source: ece.gov.nt.ca/en/curriculumrenewal.

### Nunavut
- A made-in-Nunavut curriculum grounded in Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit, one grade a year: K and 1 in 2025–26, Grades 2 and 3 in 2026–27 (the government page; some news says Grade 2 only), and full K–12 by **2036**. The other grades keep the Alberta- and NWT-based approved list.
- Sources: [gov.nu.ca, What students learn](https://www.gov.nu.ca/en/education-and-schools/what-students-learn); [Premier's announcement, March 2025](https://premier.gov.nu.ca/en/government-nunavut-announces-nunavut-curriculum-launch).

## Suggested routine
Recheck each province's curriculum site once a year in late summer (new programs nearly always start in September). The repo's `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py` make Manitoba, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick and Newfoundland and Labrador quick to refresh, and `content.test.ts` will flag any citation that no longer exists.
