                           2026-2027 Kansas Assessment Program Overview
    All tests are delivered in Kite® Student Portal (except HGSS) and include multiple-choice and technology-enhanced items. KELPA speaking
    and writing assessments are human scored. All other assessments are machine-scored. Interim and 2nd grade assessments are optional.
    General summative, Kansas English Language Proficiency Assessment (KELPA), History, Government, and Social Studies (HGSS), and
    Dynamic Learning Maps® (DLM®) assessments are mandatory.

2026-2027 Assessment Activities

Test Coordinator Webinars
Kansas District Test Coordinators (DTCs), Building Test Coordinators (BTCs), and Technology Coordinators are invited to join
monthly training webinars throughout the school year. Topics vary each month and focus on upcoming dates, deadlines,
and updates related to assessment administration. These webinars are conducted live via Zoom, but a video recording of
the webinar and a PDF of the slides used will be posted in the days following each webinar on KAP Test Coordinator
Training Webinars. Each webinar will take place on the third Thursday of the month at 1pm. Calendar invites are linked on
each date below.

Regional Test Coordinator Trainings
Annual Test Coordinator Trainings will take place in half-day sessions. Veteran District Test Coordinators are required to
attend a single session that will run from 8:30-11:30am or from 12:30-3:30pm. Register for training in Salina, Topeka, or
Maize here. Training in Sublette is linked below.

New Test Coordinator Bootcamp
All new District Test Coordinators are required to attend a full day test coordinator bootcamp. New Building Test
Coordinators are encouraged to attend as well. Required test security and ethics training will be included in this day.
Bootcamp participants do not attend the regional training. Register here.

TASC Records
Signing up students to take Interim Assessments, Instructional Mini Tests, and optional 2nd Grade Reading Assessment will
take place through the KEDS system. For students to be sent to Kite, staff section association and student section association
data need to be submitted to KEDS.

TEST Records
The information collected on TEST records to order student summative assessments will be collected in the KEDS system.
More information on how this will work will be available later. Students that will be taking one of the cPass assessments
(Comprehensive Agriculture, Plant Systems, or Animal Systems) will be ordered using the KEDS system. This information is
submitted to KEDS using the Student Assessment Resource.
