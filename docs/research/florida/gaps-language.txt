Florida English language arts (B.E.S.T. ELA): benchmarks no unit practises, Kindergarten to Grade 9

Florida's ELA benchmarks are broad: each code covers a whole area. A benchmark is counted as practised when a unit
cites it and its questions are tap-to-answer reading, vocabulary or language items. The app has no free text, voice
or handwriting, so the benchmarks below are not practised, in every grade.

Writing and handwriting (C.1.x)
- K.C.1.1 to K.C.1.5, 1.C.1.1 to 1.C.1.5, 2.C.1.1 to 2.C.1.5, 3.C.1.1 to 3.C.1.5 (printing, cursive, narratives,
  opinions, expository texts, revising): need the child's own writing. Cursive (3.C.1.1, 4.C.1.1, 5.C.1.1) needs
  handwriting.
- 4.C.1.2 to 4.C.1.5, 5.C.1.2 to 5.C.1.5, 6 to 9 C.1.2 to C.1.5: writing a narrative, claim or expository text and
  improving it. Units practise the grammar, punctuation and revising skills these draw on (C.3.1, C.1.5) with
  multiple-choice editing questions, but not the writing itself.

Communication, speaking and listening (C.2.1, EE.4.1, EE.6.1)
- C.2.1 in every grade (presenting information orally) and the K-12 expectations for discussion and voice and tone
  (ELA.K12.EE.4.1, ELA.K12.EE.6.1) need speech. Kindergarten's "Talk & Listen" is the one unit that cites EE.4.1.

Research and multimedia (C.4.1, C.5.1, C.5.2)
- Research (C.4.1) is practised only as choosing questions, sources and notes in the adopted research units; gathering
  information from several sources needs the open web.
- Using, arranging or creating multimedia, digital tools, presentations and online collaboration (C.5.1 to C.5.2)
  needs a creating tool; the media-literacy units practise reading and judging media, not making it.

Foundations: fluency (F.1.4, 612.F.2.4)
- Reading grade-level text with accuracy, automaticity and expression needs the child's voice. Sight words are
  practised (K.F.1.4, 1.F.1.4). Grade 6 to 9 foundations (612.F.2.1 to 612.F.2.4) are cited only by the spelling
  units for encoding (612.F.2.3); spoken-word and phonics work for older students is not built.
- K.F.1.1 (locating a printed word on a page, matching print to speech) is practised only in part by Book Detectives.

Reading across texts that the app can only partly show
- K.R.1.3, K.R.3.2 (retelling aloud): retelling is practised as ordering and choosing events, not speaking.
- 6.R.3.2 to 9.R.3.2 (paraphrase): practised as choosing the best paraphrase, not writing one.

Units used and not used
- Units dropped because they named Canada or other regions' places: grade 3 "fact-or-opinion", grade 4
  "punctuation-power", grade 5 "reading-detectives" (BC) kept for grades 2 and 4 only, grade 6 "close-reading" and
  "sources-bias", grade 7 "close-reading", "source-check", "text-patterns-7", "ca-g7-story-analysis" and
  "ca-g7-info-analysis", grade 8 "argument-and-media" and "grammar-and-style", grade 9 "argument-and-rhetoric",
  "revise-edit-9" and "ca-g9-rhetoric-and-sources". The Florida-only units (fl-) cover the reading benchmarks they would
  have practised.

Review needs
- All 22 units' questions and passages are original. A teacher should read the poem, allusion and epic units
  (classical references) before launch.
