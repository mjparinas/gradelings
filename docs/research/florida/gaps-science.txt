Florida science (NGSSS), Kindergarten to Grade 9: benchmarks no unit practises yet.
Every other benchmark in docs/research/florida/outcomes.json for these grades is cited by a unit in
src/content/florida/plan/science.ts or by a fl- unit in src/content/florida/units/science-*.ts.

Reasons: NoS = nature of science benchmark needing free-text investigation design, a lab or critique that a
multiple-choice practice unit cannot show (the shared units practise the vocabulary, not these skills);
A&P = Anatomy and Physiology Honors benchmarks, a course beyond Biology 1 that this app does not teach;
HS-adv = Chemistry, Physics or Biology Honors benchmarks that need quantitative or lab work not built; 
no unit = no neutral shared unit practises it yet (an adopted unit on the topic names another state or Canada, or none exists).

Notes
- Partial coverage: benchmarks covered only by an adopted unit practise the topic but not always the benchmark's exact wording. The plan lists the closest unit.
- Grade 9 is the Biology 1, Earth and Space Science and Physical Science benchmarks (SC.912) that a first science course draws on; the fl-g9 units add the Florida examples.
- Several adopted units from other frameworks name another state or Canada and were left out of Florida; Florida's own fl- units cover the same benchmarks with Florida examples where possible.
