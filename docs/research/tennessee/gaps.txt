Tennessee: standards without a unit of their own (October 24, 2026)

English language arts
- Writing (W.TTP, W.PDW, W.RBPK, W.RW) and Speaking and Listening (SL.CC, SL.PKI) in every grade: they need free text
  or voice, which the app never collects from children.
- Fluency (FL.F) in Kindergarten to Grade 5: it needs reading aloud.
- Range of reading (RL.RRTC.10, RI.RRTC.10): not a skill a question can check.

Social studies
- Cherokee, Chickasaw, Shawnee and Creek content (Grades 3, 5 and 8) is light, in the present tense and needs partner
  review before it grows.
- Grade 9 Economics is practised with the shared supply and demand, markets and personal finance units; the detailed
  macroeconomics standards (money supply, fiscal and monetary policy) are covered only lightly.
- The 2024 standards reach classrooms in 2027-28; recheck the codes against the final edition then.

Mathematics
- Grade 9 is Algebra I only; Geometry and Integrated Math I are left out.

Science
- Grade 9 covers Biology I and Physical Science; Chemistry I and Physics are not placed.
