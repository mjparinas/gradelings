Revision Date: June 2024

General Revisions
   •   Standards Document
       The title was revised from 2023 SC CCR Mathematics Standards to 2025 SC CCR
       Mathematics Standards and all instances of 2023 were changed to 2025 to align with
       the implementation date.

   •   Definition of Trapezoid (pg. 41, 51, 67, 76, 83)
       Trapezoid was defined as “A quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel sides.” This
       definition was added to the indicator insights for 3.MGSR.3.2, 4.MGSR.3.2,
       6.MGSR.1.1, 7.MGSR.1.5 (both 7th grade and compacted versions).

   •   K.PAFR.2.1 (pg.20)
       The patterns within the indicator have been italicized.

   •   1.MGSR.2.3 (pg. 24)
       In the insight, a paragraph that contained wording related to indicator 1.MGSR.2.5
       was removed as it was a copy/paste error.

   •   2nd Grade Overview (pg. 29)
       In the PAFR paragraph, on the third line, 999 was changed to 100.

   •   2.NR.3.2 (pg. 33)
       In the indicator insight, the sentence “2nd grade transitions from a number path to
       number line” was deleted.

   •   4th Grade Overview (pg. 46)
       In the MGSR paragraph on line 8, deleted “pentagons and hexagons”.

   •   5.DPSR.1.1 (pg. 58)

       hundredths place to match the limits set in 5.PAFR.1.3.

   •   5.PAFR.1.3 (pg. 61)
       An extra period at the end of the indicator was removed.
