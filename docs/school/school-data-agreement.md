# School data agreement (draft template)

> **Draft for the owner. Not legal advice and not reviewed by a lawyer. Do not sign or send until a lawyer has reviewed it and a real legal entity is named.** Placeholders are in `[brackets]`.

**Between** `[LEGAL_NAME]` ("the Provider") **and** `[School district or school]` ("the School").

## 1. What this covers

The Provider supplies Gradelings, a practice and learning service, to the School's teachers and students. This agreement covers personal information about students that the Provider handles for the School through class accounts that teachers create.

## 2. Roles

The School decides to use the service and which students use it, and is responsible for its own duties under the *Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act* (BC) `[or the applicable Ontario or Alberta Act]`, including notice to families and any consent it requires. The Provider handles student information only to provide the service to the School and for no other purpose.

## 3. What information

For each student: a first name or nickname, the class grade and curriculum, an avatar and settings, and practice results. For each teacher: an email address. Nothing else is collected from students: no email, birth year, surname, photo, voice, location or free text.

## 4. What the Provider will not do

- Use student information for advertising, profiling, or to build any product other than the service.
- Sell, rent or share student information, except with the service providers listed in the attached schedule, who are bound to protect it.
- Show advertising or load third-party trackers in the student experience.
- Try to re-identify students from de-identified or aggregated data.

## 5. Where it is stored

The service's servers run in Montréal, Canada, and its database is in Virginia, United States. The schedule lists each service provider and where it handles information. The Provider will tell the School before adding a provider or moving storage to another country, and the School may end the agreement if it objects.

## 6. Security

The Provider will keep reasonable safeguards, described in the schedule (encrypted connections, hashed credentials, access limited to the teacher who owns the class and any co-teachers the owner invites, rate-limited sign-in, scrubbed error reports). `[Add independent testing commitments once a penetration test is done.]`

## 7. Breaches

The Provider will notify the School without undue delay, and within `[__]` hours, after confirming a breach that affects its students' information, with what happened, what was involved and what is being done, so the School can meet its own duties. The Provider keeps a record of every breach.

## 8. Access, correction and deletion

The School can see, correct and delete a student's information through the teacher tools, and the Provider will help with any request it cannot complete itself within `[__]` days. A teacher removing a student, closing a class or deleting their account deletes the related student information at once from live systems, except that when the owner of a class deletes their account and the class has a co-teacher, the class passes to that co-teacher and its students stay. After 11 months without a sign-in by the teacher or a co-teacher, a linked family or a class student, the Provider warns the teacher by email. After 12 months without a sign-in, the Provider deletes the class, assignments and class-account student information; a parent-linked child is unlinked while their family information is retained by that family account. Backups are removed on the provider's cycle, within `[__]` days.

## 9. End of agreement

On ending, the Provider will delete all of the School's student information within `[__]` days and confirm in writing, unless the law requires otherwise.

## 10. Cost

The classroom tools are `[free for the School at the date of signing / priced as in the attached schedule]`. The Provider will give `[__]` days' notice of any change.

## 11. Contact, law and changes

Privacy contact: `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`. This agreement is governed by the laws of `[British Columbia / Ontario / Alberta]` and Canada. Changes must be agreed in writing.

## 12. Schools in the United States

For a school in the United States, the Provider is a "school official" under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA): it performs a service the School would otherwise do itself, is under the School's direct control for the use and upkeep of student records, uses them only for the purpose they were shared for and does not re-disclose them. The School consents on parents' behalf under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) for educational use only; the Provider uses student information for nothing else. US districts often sign the Student Data Privacy Consortium's National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA) with their state's exhibit; where they do, it replaces this template. In line with Washington's Student User Privacy in Education Rights Act (SUPER Act), the Provider does not use student information for targeted advertising, does not sell it, builds no profile of a student except for the School's educational purposes, deletes it at the School's request, keeps a security program (section 6) and gives notice before a material change to how it is handled. The same commitments meet the Oregon Student Information Protection Act (ORS 336.184) for an Oregon school and the Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (Business and Professions Code 22584) for a California school; for a California school, the School keeps ownership and control of student records, parents may review them through the School, and the Provider deletes them at the end of the agreement, as Education Code 49073.1 requires. For a Maine school, they meet the Student Information Privacy Act (Title 20-A, sections 951 to 953). Student information is stored in Virginia, United States (section 5).
