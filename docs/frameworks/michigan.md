# Michigan
- **Framework `us-mi`, slug `michigan`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Michigan K-12 Standards (Michigan Department of Education): mathematics and English language arts (2010, the Common Core: `4.NBT.1`, `RL.4.1`; Grade 9 cites the high school codes, `A-SSE.1`, `RL.9-10.1`), science (2015, NGSS codes; `MS-` under Grades 6 to 8, `HS-` under Grade 9) and social studies (2019; `3-H3.0.1`, `8-U5.2.1`, written without the spaces the document prints around the dash; Grade 9 cites the high school civics and economics codes, `C3.2.3`, `E4.1.3`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`mic()` in `src/content/michigan/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/michigan/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `docs/research/michigan/README.md`).
- **Content:** math, English language arts and science follow California's placement with Michigan codes (`src/content/michigan/plan/`), copying the same units from the same files (each grade file's `POOL`); California's science units that name California places are replaced by `mi-` copies with Michigan examples, and other borrowed units that name another state's places by filtered copies (`localCopy`). Social studies follows Michigan's grade themes: Myself and Others (K, with the flag and Michigan's symbols), Families and Schools (1), the Local Community (2, with local government), **Michigan Studies (3, all Michigan's own: land and lakes, the Anishinaabek, early Michigan to statehood, its economy and government)**, United States Studies with Michigan history from statehood (4: lumber, copper, iron and cars, migration and the Underground Railroad, the market economy), Integrated United States History to 1791 (5, with the Eastern Woodland peoples), Global Geography (6), World History and Geography to 1500 (7), Integrated United States History from 1787 to 1877 (8, with Michigan and the nation) and civics and economics (9, with governing Michigan under its 1963 constitution and personal finance). Michigan-only units (`mi-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) are in `src/content/michigan/units/`. No borrowed unit may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`) outside US history and civics, and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`michigan.test.ts`). Anishinaabe and other Native content (the Ojibwe, Odawa and Potawatomi, Michigan's 12 federally recognized Tribes, treaty fishing rights) is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are the M-STEP (English language arts and math in grades 3 to 7, science and social studies in 5, 8 and 11), the PSAT 8/9 in grades 8 and 9, the PSAT 10, the Michigan Merit Examination (with the SAT) in grade 11, MI-Access, and early literacy and math benchmark assessments in Kindergarten to Grade 2; the Read by Grade Three Law (no required retention since 2023) is on the state tests guide.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/michigan/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, MDE's Social and Emotional Learning Competencies and Indicators (2017, five competencies, not graded; `/guides/michigan/sel-standards/`) and the state tests (`/guides/michigan/state-tests/`; dates point to MDE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Student Online Personal Protection Act (Act 368 of 2016, MCL 388.1291 to 388.1295) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Michigan.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as an operator under Michigan's Student Online Personal Protection Act (Act 368 of 2016, MCL 388.1291 to 388.1295): no targeted advertising, no profile beyond the school's purposes, no sale or rental, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), reasonable security, and deletion when the school asks. MCL 380.1136 (protection of pupil privacy) binds the district itself, including its directory-information opt-out form for families. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- Michigan: Anishinaabe content is light and needs partner review before it grows; English language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency, and the social studies public discourse and civic action standards (`P3`, `P4`), have no unit (see `docs/research/michigan/README.md`).
