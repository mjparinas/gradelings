# Alabama
- **Framework `us-al`, slug `alabama`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Alabama Course of Study: Mathematics (2019), English Language Arts (2021), Science (2023) and Social Studies (2024, in schools from 2026-27), cited by grade and standard number (`4.12`, Kindergarten `K.3`, recurring English language arts standards `4.R2`). Grade 9 math is Geometry with Data Analysis (`GDA.5`), Alabama's standard Grade 9 course, with Algebra I with Probability (`AIP.5`); Grade 9 science is Biology (`BIO.3`); middle school science is Earth and Space (6), Life (7) and Physical (8). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`ala()` in `src/content/alabama/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/alabama/outcomes.json` (the ALSDE PDFs are committed in `source/`; `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py` rebuild it; record: `docs/research/alabama/README.md`).
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`src/content/alabama/plan/`, pools in `pools/<subject>-<grade>.ts`), plus Alabama-only units (`al-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/alabama/units/`. No unit Alabama borrows may name Washington State, Oregon, California, New York places, Texas, Minnesota or Maine as a place (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`; `copyOf` makes filtered `al-` copies), and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed.
- **Social studies** follows the 2024 course sequence: family and community (K), community and state (1), state and nation (2), **Alabama in American History and Government I to III (Grades 3 to 5**, mostly Alabama's own units: geography and Indigenous peoples to the early 1700s, colonies to 1900, the twentieth century to the present), US beginnings and civic foundations (6), and world history and geography (7 ancient, 8 Middle Ages to the Enlightenment, 9 Age of Revolution to the present; Alabama teaches world history, not civics, in Grade 9). Muscogee (Creek), Cherokee, Choctaw, Chickasaw and Poarch Creek content is light and in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are ACAP (English language arts and math in grades 2 to 8, science in 4, 6 and 8); the Alabama Literacy Act's 3rd grade reading rule is on the state tests guide.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/alabama/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, the learning practices of each subject (`/guides/alabama/learning-practices/`; Alabama has no statewide SEL standards) and ACAP (`/guides/alabama/state-tests/`; dates point to ALSDE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** Alabama has no student privacy statute of its own beyond FERPA; `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` say we work under the district's agreement (the State Board's Data Use Governance Policy) with the same commitments as the other states'.

## Schools

- **Alabama law.** Alabama has no student privacy statute of its own beyond FERPA. The State Board of Education's Data Use Governance Policy has every district adopt a local student records governance and use policy and share data with service providers only under a signed agreement, so an Alabama district will ask for its own agreement or the NDPA. `/privacy/` states the same commitments as for the other states: no targeted advertising, no sale, no profiling beyond the school's purpose, sharing only as the law allows, a security program and deletion when the school asks. The Alabama Personal Data Protection Act (2026, in force May 1, 2027) leaves out data FERPA covers, and treats a known child's data under COPPA.
