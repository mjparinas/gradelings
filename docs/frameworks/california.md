# California
- **Framework `us-ca`, slug `california`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like Washington and Oregon. Standards are CDE's: CA CCSS for mathematics (`4.NBT.1`, California additions such as `2.NBT.7.1`; Grade 9 is the Algebra I course, `A-SSE.1`, `F-IF.4`), CA CCSS for ELA/literacy (`RL.4.1`; Grade 9 uses the 9–10 band, `RL.9-10.1`), CA NGSS with California's integrated middle school placement (`MS-LS1-1` in Grade 6, `MS-PS1-2` in Grade 7, `MS-PS2-1` in Grade 8, `MS-ETS1` in all three) and the History–Social Science Content Standards (`4.2.1`; Grade 9 cites the Principles of American Democracy, `12.1` to `12.10`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`cal()` in `src/content/california/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/california/outcomes.json` (rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `docs/research/california/README.md`).
- **Pre-K (Transitional Kindergarten):** shown as "Transitional Kindergarten" (`preKName`). It uses the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), each citing the California Preschool/Transitional Kindergarten Learning Foundations (2024) as "<domain> <number>" (`Mathematics 1.3`; `src/content/california/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/california/prek/outcomes.json` (the At-a-Glance PDF in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; Washington's, Oregon's and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`californiaGrade` in `kit.ts`; Washington's grade first in each pool, then Oregon's, whose courses already hold the units Oregon lifts from other grades), plus California-only units (`ca-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/california/units/`. No unit California uses may mention Washington State or Oregon as a place (George Washington, Washington, D.C. and the Oregon Trail are fine; the 50 states and capitals unit is the one exception), and Washington's and Oregon's Tribal units are never borrowed. Where a Washington or Oregon unit fits except for a few items, California has a filtered `ca-` copy (`copyOf` in `units/kit.ts` drops questions naming either state, a Pacific Northwest place, Tribes or treaties, and adds California items). `ca-` units carry only `us-` standards. Its own math units (the California additions to the Common Core, such as `ca-g5-prime-factors`, and the standards no shared unit practised) have lessons in `src/content/lessons/`. Every math, science (Grade 9: all three high school courses) and history–social science standard, and every ELA standard but writing, speaking and listening, fluency and "read a range of texts" (which need free text or voice), has a unit; `california.test.ts` checks it, so a new standard needs a unit in the same change.
- **Social studies:** Kindergarten to Grade 3 adopt general units and add California ones (symbols, calendars, heroes, the local region, California Indian nations, local history and government). Grade 4 (California, a changing state: regions, California Indian nations, missions and ranchos, the Gold Rush and statehood, growth, state government) is all California's own. The mission units say plainly that the missions caused great harm to California Indian peoples, and name the nations as living communities. Grades 5 to 8 follow the H-SS course titles (US history to 1850, ancient civilizations, medieval and early modern times, US history to 1914) with copies and own units; Grade 9 is civics (Principles of American Democracy), which California schools usually teach in Grade 12. California Indian content is light and in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; identity, immigration and civil rights content stays factual and neutral.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as Washington and Oregon. State tests are CAASPP.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/california/guides.ts`, US English, registered in `GUIDES`): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, CDE's SEL Guiding Principles and five Transformative SEL competencies (voluntary guidance, not graded; served at `/guides/california/sel-standards/`) and CAASPP (`/guides/california/state-tests/`: Smarter Balanced ELA and math in grades 3 to 8 and 11, CAST science in grades 5 and 8 and once in high school, the Education Code 60615 written exemption; dates point to CDE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our SOPIPA (Business and Professions Code 22584) and Education Code 49073.1 commitments next to Washington's and Oregon's.

## Schools

- **California law.** `/privacy/` states the matching commitments under SOPIPA (Business and Professions Code 22584): no targeted advertising, no sale, no profiling beyond the school's purpose, sharing only as the law allows, a security program and deletion when the school asks. A California district's contract must carry the terms in Education Code 49073.1 (AB 1584); `school-data-agreement.md` and the NDPA's California exhibit cover them.
