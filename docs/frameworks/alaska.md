# Alaska
- **Framework `us-ak`, slug `alaska`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Alaska content standards (Department of Education and Early Development): 2012 math (Common Core-style codes without cluster letters, `4.NBT.1`, with Alaska's own numbering and additions such as `1.CC.*` and time zones in `4.MD.4`; Grade 9 cites the high school codes, `A-SSE.1`), 2012 ELA (`RL.4.1`, `W.4.2`, `L.4.4`; Grade 9 cites `RL.9-10.1`), 2019 science (NGSS codes; `MS-` under Grades 6 to 8, `HS-` under Grade 9) and 2024 social studies (`SS.4.1.15.1`; bands `SS.K-2.x.y`, `SS.3-5`, `SS.6-8`; Grade 9 cites `SS.9-12.x.y` and the Alaska History course, `SS.AKH.5.8.1`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`ak()` in `src/content/alaska/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/alaska/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `gradelings-research/alaska/README.md`).
- **Content:** math, English language arts and science follow California's placement with Alaska codes (`src/content/alaska/plan/`), copying the same units from the same files (each grade file's `POOL`). Social studies follows Alaska's grade themes (Me and My World, My Place in the World, My Local Community, Alaska's Past, Present, and Future in 3, U.S. Cultures and Regions in 4, Foundations and History of the U.S. in 5, **Alaska Studies and Geography in 6**, World History and Geography in 7, U.S. History and Civics in 8); Grade 9 is civics, with the Alaska Constitution, statehood and ANCSA, and the resource economy and Permanent Fund. It adopts general units from BC, the provinces and the other states, plus Alaska-only units (`ak-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/alaska/units/` (social studies in Grades 2, 3, 4 and 6 to 9, among them Russian America in 7, the first peoples, the purchase and Tribal, state and federal governments in 8, and Alaska Native rights and sovereignty in 9). Every science standard and every ELA standard but writing, speaking and listening, fluency and reading range has a unit (`alaska.test.ts`); where a California unit names California places, Alaska uses an `ak-` copy with Alaska examples (`copyOf` in `units/kit.ts`). No unit Alaska uses may name Washington State, Oregon or California as a place (US history mentions are fine), and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`alaska.test.ts`). Alaska Native content is light, in the present tense, about living communities and governments, with nothing sacred or ceremonial.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale (districts choose; many use standards-based 1 to 4 reporting), so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are AK STAR (English language arts and math, grades 3 to 9) and the Alaska Science Assessment (grades 5, 8 and 10).
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/alaska/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, the Cultural Standards for Students A to E from the Alaska Standards for Culturally-Responsive Schools (`/guides/alaska/cultural-standards/`, not graded) and the state tests (`/guides/alaska/state-tests/`; dates point to DEED). No French guide.
- **Schools:** Alaska has no student online privacy statute of its own, so `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` give Alaska schools the same commitments as the other states, with breach notice under the Alaska Personal Information Protection Act (AS 45.48).

## Schools

- **Alaska.** Alaska has no student online privacy statute of its own. `/privacy/` gives Alaska schools the same commitments as other states (no targeted advertising, no sale, no profiling beyond the school's purpose, sharing only with the listed providers, a security program, deletion when the school asks) and says we tell the school about a breach affecting its students, in line with the Alaska Personal Information Protection Act (AS 45.48). FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.
