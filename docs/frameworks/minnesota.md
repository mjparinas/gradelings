# Minnesota
- **Framework `us-mn`, slug `minnesota`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Minnesota K-12 Academic Standards (MDE, in rule): 2022 math (`4.3.5.12`; Kindergarten is grade 0; Grade 9 cites the 9-11 benchmarks), 2020 ELA (`4.1.4.2`), 2019 science (`6E.3.1.1.2`; middle school is by domain: Earth and space in Grade 6, life science in 7, physical science in 8) and 2021 social studies (`6.4.19.3`, five strands including Ethnic Studies). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`mns()` in `src/content/minnesota/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/minnesota/outcomes.json` (rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; education.mn.gov blocks scripts, so the sources are the Legislative Reference Library's copies; record: `gradelings-research/minnesota/README.md`).
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; the other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`minnesotaGrade` in `kit.ts`; pools in `pools/<subject>-<grade>.ts`), plus Minnesota-only units (`mn-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/minnesota/units/`. No unit Minnesota borrows may name Washington State, Oregon or California as a place, or the places other states' units are built around (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`; the 50 states and capitals unit is the one exception), and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed; filtered `mn-` copies (`copyOf`) fill the gaps. `minnesota.test.ts` checks both.
- **Social studies** follows the standards' grade themes: Family and Community (K), Communities and Culture (1), People and the Environment (2), the Ancient World (3), the Contemporary World (4), Early American Studies (5), **Minnesota Studies (6, all Minnesota's own)**, United States Studies (7) and Global Studies (8); Grade 9 is civics and economics. Dakota and Anishinaabe content is light and in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are the MCA (and MTAS).
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/minnesota/guides.ts`, US English, registered in `GUIDES`): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, MDE's five SEL competencies (not graded; `/guides/minnesota/sel-standards/`) and the MCA (`/guides/minnesota/state-tests/`: reading and math in grades 3 to 8, reading in 10, math in 11, science in 5, 8 and once in high school, and the parent refusal form; dates point to MDE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Minnesota Statutes 13.32 (subdivisions 13 and 14, technology providers) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Minnesota law.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as a technology provider under Minnesota Statutes 13.32, subdivisions 13 and 14 (added in 2022): student information belongs to the school, no sale, sharing or dissemination beyond what the law allows, no commercial use (marketing or advertising to students or parents included), a security program, breach information to the school so it can meet section 13.055, and deletion or return within 90 days after the contract ends unless renewal is expected. Subdivision 14 bars monitoring location, camera, microphone, keystrokes or browsing on a school-issued device; the app uses none of these. A technology provider is also bound by section 13.05, subdivision 11 (a government contractor's duties under the Data Practices Act).
