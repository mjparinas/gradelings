# Wisconsin
- **Framework `us-wi`, slug `wisconsin`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are Wisconsin's academic standards (Department of Public Instruction, from its CASE server wisestandards.dpi.wi.gov): mathematics (2021; `M.4.NBT.A.1`, Wisconsin's own numbering of some Common Core standards; Grade 9 cites `M.9-12.A.SSE.A.1`, `M.9-12.F-IF.B.4`), English language arts (2020; `ELA.4.R.1`, one Reading strand, `ELA.2.RF.3`, `ELA.4.L.1`; Grade 9 cites `ELA.9-10.*`), science (2017; `SCI.4.PS.3`, learning priorities `SCI.4.PS.3.A`; middle school `SCI.m.*` in Grades 6 to 8, high school `SCI.h.*` under Grade 9), social studies (2018; standards `SS.Hist2` in every grade, indicators by band `SS.Hist2.b.i` with `e`, `i`, `m`, `h` or a grade, `SS.Geog2.d.4-5`) and personal financial literacy (2020; `PFL.i.SI.1.a.1`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`wis()` in `src/content/wisconsin/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/wisconsin/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; the CASE server leaves out some ELA standards, which `build_outcomes.py` adds from DPI's Word document; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `gradelings-research/wisconsin/README.md`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), cited by the Wisconsin Model Early Learning Standards' performance standards with the domain (`V.B.EL.1`; the standards have no social studies domain, so social units cite social and emotional development and mathematical thinking) (`src/content/wisconsin/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/wisconsin/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** math, English language arts and science follow California's placement with Wisconsin codes (`src/content/wisconsin/plan/`), copying the same units from the same files (each grade file's `POOL`); California's, Washington's and Oregon's science units that name their places are `wi-` copies with Wisconsin examples (`units/science-g*.ts`), and other borrowed units that name another state's places are filtered copies (`localCopy`, `units/copies-g*.ts`). Borrowed social studies units cite Wisconsin's standards; Wisconsin's own units (`wi-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) cite indicators. No borrowed unit may name another state's places outside US history and civics (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`), and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`wisconsin.test.ts`).
- **Social studies:** Wisconsin leaves the order to districts, so communities in Kindergarten to Grade 3 (Wisconsin's symbols in 1; its land, communities and local government in 3), **Wisconsin studies in Grade 4 (all Wisconsin's own: regions, the eleven First Nations of Wisconsin, early Wisconsin and statehood, the growing state, a timeline, state government and the economy)**, early US history (5), world geography and ancient civilizations (6), world history (7), US history to Reconstruction with Wisconsin and the US and Wisconsin constitutions (8) and civics with the 1848 constitution, personal finance and economics (9). First Nations content follows Act 31, is light, in the present tense, about sovereign living nations, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; removal (the Black Hawk War, the Sandy Lake Tragedy) is told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are the Forward Exam (English language arts and math in grades 3 to 8, science in 4 and 8, social studies in 4, 8 and 10; Developing, Approaching, Meeting, Advanced), PreACT Secure (9 and 10) and the ACT (11); graduation needs the civics test (Wis. Stat. 118.33) and, from the class of 2028, a half credit of personal finance (2023 Act 60).
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/wisconsin/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, DPI's six Guiding Principles for Teaching and Learning (`/guides/wisconsin/guiding-principles/`, not graded) and the Forward Exam (`/guides/wisconsin/state-tests/`; dates point to DPI). No French guide.
- **Schools:** Wisconsin has no student online privacy statute for technology providers (pupil records law, Wis. Stat. 118.125, binds schools), so `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` give Wisconsin schools the same commitments as the other states', with breach notice to the school without unreasonable delay and within 45 days (Wis. Stat. 134.98). Keep that true.

## Schools

- **Wisconsin.** Wisconsin has no student online privacy statute binding technology providers (pupil records law, Wis. Stat. 118.125, binds the school), so `/privacy/` gives Wisconsin schools the same commitments as the other states', under the school's agreement and FERPA and COPPA. Wisconsin's breach notification law (Wis. Stat. 134.98) has a business that keeps data for another tell its owner of a breach as soon as practicable, and the owner notify residents within a reasonable time, not more than 45 days; we promise the school notice without unreasonable delay and within 45 days at most. Offer the district's own agreement or the NDPA if it asks.

## Known gaps

- Wisconsin: English language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit; Grade 9 math covers algebra and functions only; the Holocaust and genocide teaching Act 30 requires has no unit of its own; First Nations content is light and needs partner review (see `gradelings-research/wisconsin/README.md`).
