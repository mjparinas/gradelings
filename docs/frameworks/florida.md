# Florida
- **Framework `us-fl`, slug `florida`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Florida Department of Education's, as CPALMS publishes them: the B.E.S.T. Standards for Mathematics (`MA.4.NSO.1.1`; Grade 9 is Algebra 1 and Geometry, `MA.912.AR.3.1`), the B.E.S.T. Standards for English Language Arts (`ELA.4.R.1.1`; the K-12 expectations `ELA.K12.EE.*` count in every grade and `ELA.612.F.*` in Grades 6 to 9), the Next Generation Sunshine State Standards for science (`SC.4.L.16.1`; Grade 9 cites the 9-12 benchmarks, `SC.912.L.14.1`) and the state social studies standards (`SS.4.A.1.1`; `SS.68.*` counts in Grades 6 to 8; Grade 9 is World History, `SS.912.W.1.1`, with the other 9-12 strands it draws on). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`fl()` in `src/content/florida/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/florida/outcomes.json` (the books are committed in `source/`; `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py` rebuild it from the books and CPALMS's benchmark files; fldoe.org blocks scripted downloads, so the sources are CPALMS's copies; record: `docs/research/florida/README.md`).
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`src/content/florida/plan/`, pools in `pools/<subject>-<grade>.ts`, `floridaGrade` in `kit.ts`), plus Florida-only units (`fl-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/florida/units/`, with lessons in `src/content/lessons/`. Math and English language arts follow California's placement with B.E.S.T. codes where the grade matches; science and social studies follow Florida's own grade placement. Social studies follows the state's sequence: Florida symbols, civics, economics and geography in Kindergarten to Grade 3, **Florida history in Grade 4 (all Florida's own)**, American history (5), world history (6), civics and government (7, with the Civics end-of-course exam), American history to Reconstruction (8, whose standards include a large financial literacy strand) and World History (9). No unit Florida borrows may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`; US history and civics grades may name states where the history does) or be another state's Tribal unit (`florida.test.ts`). Seminole Tribe of Florida and Miccosukee Tribe of Indians of Florida content is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; slavery, removal, the Seminole Wars and segregation are told plainly. Florida's wording for the Gulf of America is used.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale for Kindergarten to Grade 8, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are FAST (reading in grades 3 to 10, math in grades 3 to 8, three windows a year), the writing test, the Statewide Science Assessment (grades 5 and 8) and end-of-course exams; the grade 3 reading rule (section 1008.25, Florida Statutes) is on the state tests guide.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/florida/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, the thinking and reasoning standards (MTRs, English language arts Expectations, Nature of Science; `/guides/florida/thinking-standards/`; Florida has no statewide SEL standards) and the state tests (`/guides/florida/state-tests/`; dates point to FLDOE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Florida Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (section 1006.1494, Florida Statutes) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Florida.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as an operator under Florida's Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (section 1006.1494, Florida Statutes): no targeted advertising, no sharing, selling or renting, no profile beyond the school's purposes, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), collection limited to what the service needs, a security program, and deletion when the school asks, including within 90 days of the district telling us a student is no longer enrolled. State Board of Education Rule 6A-1.09550 sets what a district's student data policy and vendor contracts must include; offer the district's own agreement or the NDPA with Florida's exhibit if it asks. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- Florida: English language arts benchmarks that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit; Grade 9 social studies covers only World History and a few 9-12 strands (the rest of 912.HC, 912.FL, 912.AA and 912.G is uncovered), and most Grade 8 financial literacy benchmarks (8.FL) have no unit; Seminole and Miccosukee content is light and needs partner review (see `docs/research/florida/README.md` and its `gaps-*.txt`).
