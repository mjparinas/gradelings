# Massachusetts
- **Framework `us-ma`, slug `massachusetts`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Massachusetts Curriculum Frameworks (DESE): mathematics (2017; `4.NBT.A.1`, Grade 9 is the Model Algebra I course, `AI.A-SSE.A.1`), English language arts and literacy (2017; `RL.4.1`, Grade 9 cites `RL.9-10.1`), science and technology/engineering (2016; NGSS codes with Massachusetts additions marked `(MA)`; middle school standards placed by grade, `6.MS-LS1-1`, and the engineering band carries the grade, `3.3-5-ETS1-1`; Grade 9 cites `HS-`) and history and social science (2018; topic and number, `3.T4.1`, lettered parts `7.T1b.2`; Grade 9 cites United States History I, `USI.T1.4`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`mas()` in `src/content/massachusetts/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/massachusetts/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `docs/research/massachusetts/README.md`). Its own units use the prefix `ma-g` (`ma-gk-` in Kindergarten), because Core French already has a unit called `ma-classe`.
- **Content:** math and English language arts follow California's placement with Massachusetts codes; science follows Massachusetts's own grade placement (`src/content/massachusetts/plan/`, each grade file's `POOL`). Social studies follows the framework's grade themes, with **Massachusetts and New England in Grade 3** (mostly Massachusetts's own units: geography, cities and towns, the Wampanoag and Plymouth, the Massachusetts Bay Colony, the Revolution in Massachusetts), North American regions in 4, US history to the civil rights movement in 5, world geography and ancient civilizations in 6 and 7, US and Massachusetts government in 8 and United States History I in 9. Massachusetts-only units (`bankUnit` in `own.ts`) and filtered copies of other states' units with Massachusetts examples (`localCopy`, `units/copies-g*.ts`) are in `src/content/massachusetts/units/`. No unit Massachusetts borrows may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`) outside US history and civics, and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`massachusetts.test.ts`). Wampanoag, Nipmuc and other Native content is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are MCAS (English language arts and math in grades 3 to 8 and 10, science in 5, 8 and once in high school; MCAS-Alt; four achievement levels). Since Question 2 (November 2024), passing MCAS is no longer a graduation requirement.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/massachusetts/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, the learning practices built into the frameworks (`/guides/massachusetts/learning-practices/`; Massachusetts has no SEL standards in its frameworks) and MCAS (`/guides/massachusetts/state-tests/`; dates point to DESE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** Massachusetts has no student online privacy statute for technology providers, so `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` give Massachusetts schools the same commitments as the other states', under 603 CMR 23.00, and say we tell a school of a breach as soon as practicable and without unreasonable delay (M.G.L. chapter 93H). Keep that true.

## Schools

- **Massachusetts.** Massachusetts has no student online privacy statute binding technology providers (a student data privacy bill is before the legislature), so `/privacy/` gives Massachusetts schools the same commitments as the other states', under the school's agreement, the student records regulations (603 CMR 23.00) and FERPA and COPPA. Massachusetts's breach law (M.G.L. chapter 93H, section 3(a)) has a party that holds data for another tell the data's owner as soon as practicable and without unreasonable delay; `/privacy/` says we tell the school that way. Many districts sign the Massachusetts Student Privacy Alliance's data privacy agreement; offer the district's own agreement or that one.

## Known gaps

- Massachusetts: English language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit; Grade 9 social studies covers United States History I only; Wampanoag, Nipmuc and other Native content is light and needs partner review (see `docs/research/massachusetts/README.md`).
