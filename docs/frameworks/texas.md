# Texas
- **Framework `us-tx`, slug `texas`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts and reading, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS), 19 TAC Chapters 110 (ELAR 2017), 111 (math 2012), 112 (science 2021) and 113 (social studies, 2022 edition), cited by student expectation: `3.4(A)` (Kindergarten `K.2(B)`). Grade 9 is Algebra I (`A.5(A)`), English I (`E1.6(B)`), Biology (`B.5(A)`) and, by the US civics decision, United States Government (`GOV.1(A)`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`tx()` in `src/content/texas/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/texas/outcomes.json` (the TEA chapter PDFs are committed in `source/`; `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py` rebuild it; record: `docs/research/texas/README.md`).
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt`, plus Texas-only units (`tx-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/texas/units/`. Each grade and subject has one file in `src/content/texas/courses/` holding the unit files it copies from (`POOL`) and its own units (`OWN`), and its plan in `src/content/texas/plan/` (Grades 4 and 7 social studies, Texas history, in `plan/social-texas.ts`). No unit Texas uses may name Washington State, Oregon or California as a place, outside US history and civics (`texas.test.ts`); `copyOf` in `units/kit.ts` makes filtered `tx-` copies. Other states' Tribal units are never borrowed. Grades 4 and 7 (Texas history) are Texas's own; American Indian content is light and in the present tense (the Alabama-Coushatta, the Kickapoo Traditional Tribe of Texas and Ysleta del Sur Pueblo are Texas's federally recognized tribes), and the mission units say plainly that the missions harmed many American Indian people.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are STAAR, being replaced in grades 3 to 8 by three shorter tests a year from 2027–28 (House Bill 8), so the guide points to TEA.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/texas/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, the TEKS process skills (`/guides/texas/process-skills/`; Texas has no statewide SEL standards) and STAAR (`/guides/texas/state-tests/`). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Texas Education Code chapter 32, subchapter D (§§32.151–32.157) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Texas law.** `/privacy/` states the matching commitments under Texas Education Code chapter 32, subchapter D (sections 32.151 to 32.157): no targeted advertising, no sale or rental, no profiling beyond the school's purpose, sharing only as the law allows, a security program and deletion when the school asks.
