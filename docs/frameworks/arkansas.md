# Arkansas
- **Framework `us-ar`, slug `arkansas`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Arkansas Academic Standards (Arkansas Department of Education, DESE): 2023 math (`4.NPV.3`, `K.CAR.2`; Grade 9 is Algebra I, `A1.LFE.3`), 2023 English language arts (`4.RC.8.RL`, `K.FR.4.CP`, vocabulary without a sub-strand `4.V.2`), the K-12 Science Standards (NGSS codes placed by grade, `4-PS3-1`, `6-LS1-1`, `7-PS1-2`, `8-PS2-1`; Grade 9 cites the high school courses, `BI-LS1-1`, `PSI-PS2-1`) and the 2022 Social Studies Academic Standards (`C.1.4.2`, `H.2.6.1`; the Grades 7-8 Arkansas History course `H.1.ARH.4` counts in both grades; Grade 9 cites Civics, `C.3.CIV.5`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`ark()` in `src/content/arkansas/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/arkansas/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; the DESE site leaves out its intermediate certificate, so `fetch.sh` adds it; record: `docs/research/arkansas/README.md`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), cited by the Arkansas Child Development and Early Learning Standards' goals (`MT1.1`) (`src/content/arkansas/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/arkansas/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`src/content/arkansas/plan/`, pools in `pools/<subject>-<grade>.ts`), plus Arkansas-only units (`ar-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/arkansas/units/`. Social studies follows the 2022 standards: Kindergarten to Grade 4 integrated, with Arkansas symbols, goods, landmarks, regions, government and history in Arkansas's own units; World Geography (5); World History (6); United States history to about 1850 (7) and since 1850 (8), each with Arkansas History units; Civics (9), with Arkansas's government. No unit Arkansas borrows may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`, which also covers Alabama, Alaska and Arizona; `copyOf` makes filtered `ar-` copies), and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`arkansas.test.ts`). Caddo, Osage and Quapaw content is light and in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale for Kindergarten to Grade 8 (high school courses use the uniform grading scale), so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are ATLAS (English language arts, math and science, grades 3 to 10, no 9th grade science; checks in Kindergarten to Grade 2); the LEARNS Act's 3rd grade reading rule is on the state tests guide.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/arkansas/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the learning practices built into the standards (`/guides/arkansas/learning-practices/`; Arkansas has no statewide SEL standards) and ATLAS (`/guides/arkansas/state-tests/`; dates point to DESE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Arkansas Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (Arkansas Code 6-18-109) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Arkansas.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as an operator under Arkansas's Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (Arkansas Code 6-18-109): no targeted advertising, no sale, no profile beyond the school's purposes, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), a security program, and deletion when the school asks. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- Arkansas: Caddo, Osage and Quapaw content is light, and some math and English language arts standards have no unit yet (see `docs/research/arkansas/README.md`).
