# New York
- **Framework `us-ny`, slug `new-york`** Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are NYSED's: the Next Generation Mathematics Learning Standards (`NY-4.NBT.1`; Grade 9 is Algebra I, `AI-A.SSE.1`), the Next Generation ELA Learning Standards (`4R1`, `4W2`, `4RF3`; one Reading strand for literature and informational text; `4L1`/`4L2` are the banded grammar and spelling standards; Grade 9 cites `9-10R1`), the P-12 Science Learning Standards (NGSS codes; middle school `MS-` is a 6-8 band valid in Grades 6, 7 and 8; Grade 9 cites `HS-`) and the K-12 Social Studies Framework (`4.2`, `4.2a`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`nys()` in `src/content/new-york/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/new-york/outcomes.json` (rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `docs/research/new-york/README.md`).
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; Washington's, Oregon's, California's and the provinces' units via `adopt`, plus New York-only units (`ny-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/new-york/units/`. Each grade's subjects keep their pools in `pools/<subject>-<grade>.ts` and their own units in `units/<subject>-<grade>.ts`, so a grade's download carries only the files it copies from. No unit New York borrows may name Washington State, Oregon or California as a place (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`; George Washington, Washington, D.C., the Oregon Trail and the California Gold Rush are fine), and other states' Tribal and California Indian units are never borrowed; where a unit fits except for a few items, New York has a filtered `ny-` copy (`copyOf`).
- **Social studies** follows the framework's grade themes: Self and Others (K), families now and long ago (1), communities in the United States (2), communities around the world (3), New York State history and government (4, all New York's own), the Western Hemisphere (5), the Eastern Hemisphere (6), History of the United States and New York (7 and 8) and **Global History and Geography I (9)**, the course New York teaches in grade 9 (not civics, as in the other states). Haudenosaunee, Lenape and other Native nations' content is light and in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are the Grades 3-8 ELA and math tests, science in grades 5 and 8, and Regents Exams in high school.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/new-york/guides.ts`, US English, registered in `GUIDES`): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, NYSED's Social Emotional Learning Benchmarks (voluntary, not graded; `/guides/new-york/sel-standards/`) and the state tests (`/guides/new-york/state-tests/`; dates point to NYSED). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Education Law 2-d and Part 121 commitments next to the other states'. Before signing with a New York district, confirm encryption at rest and set the breach-notice window (Part 121 asks for 7 calendar days); `docs/school/README.md` says so.

## Schools

- **New York law.** `/privacy/` states our commitments under Education Law 2-d and Part 121 of the Commissioner's regulations: no sale, no use or release for marketing or commercial purposes, use only for the school's educational purposes, sharing only with the listed service providers, a security program, deletion when the school asks and telling the school about a breach affecting its students. Every New York district contract must carry the district's Parents' Bill of Rights for Data Privacy and Security and supplemental information about the product, and Part 121 asks a contractor for a data security and privacy plan, encryption in transit and at rest, security practices aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and breach notice to the district within 7 calendar days of discovery. Encryption at rest (Turso) is still a **(confirm)** item, and the breach window in `school-data-agreement.md` section 7 is blank; settle both before signing with a New York district.
