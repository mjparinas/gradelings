# Saskatchewan
- **Framework `ca-sk`, slug `saskatchewan`**, Prekindergarten and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, language, science, social studies and both French subjects (French Immersion Kindergarten to Grade 9; Core French Levels 1 to 9, which this app teaches in Grades 1 to 9). Standards are outcomes cited by code, checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/saskatchewan/outcomes.json` (record: `gradelings-research/saskatchewan/README.md`).
- **Pre-K (Prekindergarten):** shown as "Prekindergarten" (`preKName`), the school program for some three- and four-year-olds. It uses the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), each citing an experience of Essential Learning Experiences for Three-, Four- and Five-Year-Olds (`Intellectual: Exploring Numeracy`; `src/content/saskatchewan/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/saskatchewan/prek/outcomes.json` (PDF in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Scoring:** no single provincial scale, so one four-level scheme (Beginning, Approaching, Meeting, Exemplary) with the usual kid labels. Same "practice, not a report-card mark" rule.
- **French** (`src/content/saskatchewan/french.ts`, `french-units.ts`; sources and record in `docs/research/saskatchewan/`): the Ministry offers Core French and French Immersion from Kindergarten to Grade 12 (saskatchewan.ca, "Studying French in Saskatchewan"). Core French is curriculum Levels 1 to 9 (curriculum.gov.sk.ca; a level is a language level, not a grade, and each school division decides when students start), taught here with the level matching the grade, and cites the four goals of each level (`4.CS.1`, `5.LK.1`, `6.C.1`). Immersion is *Français en immersion* Grades 3 to 9 (strands `3.PÉ.2`, `5.VC.3`) plus the interdisciplinary program for Kindergarten to Grade 2 (`É-AC.4`, `AP-GV.1`), cited by those codes. BC French units are shared with Saskatchewan citations; Grade 4 Core French reuses Ontario's Grade 4 units; Core French Grades 1 to 3, two Grade 4 units and the culture units (`sk-cf4-fransaskois`, `sk-cf6-noms-de-lieux`, `sk-cf8-contributions`, `sk-imm5-cultures`, `sk-imm8-diversite`) are written for Saskatchewan (`sk-` ids). The Conseil des écoles fransaskoises (Francophone first-language) program is not built. Fransaskois, Métis and First Nations content is light and needs partner review. There is a French guide at `/guides/saskatchewan/french/`. To refresh the outcomes, run `gradelings-research/saskatchewan/build_french_outcomes.py` (it also rewrites the generated `french-outcomes.ts`).
- **Shared units:** BC units via `share(...)`, Ontario units via `reuse(...)`, plus Saskatchewan-only units in `src/content/saskatchewan/`. Known gaps (partly covered outcomes, French strands without a unit, review needs) are listed in the research README.
- **First Nations, Métis and treaty content** is light.

## Known gaps

- Saskatchewan French: no unit practises Immersion listening (`CO`) or the language-appreciation outcomes (`VC.1`, `VC.2`), or most Core French General Language Strategies (`GL`); and Core French is taught by grade, so a child who starts it later than Grade 1 meets a higher level (see `gradelings-research/saskatchewan/README.md`).
