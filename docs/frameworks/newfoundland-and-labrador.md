# Newfoundland and Labrador
- **Framework `ca-nl`, slug `newfoundland-and-labrador`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, language, science, social studies and Core French (Grades 4 to 9). **No French Immersion**: its curriculum guides are only on the department's login-protected eNLightenED site. The English program follows the Atlantic Canada curricula. The only public curriculum documents are the **foundation documents**, which state outcomes for the end of Grade 3, Grade 6 and Grade 9, so a unit cites a **strand and key stage** ("Operation sense (by Grade 6)"; Kindergarten to Grade 3 cite the Grade 3 stage, Grades 4 to 6 the Grade 6 stage, Grades 7 to 9 the Grade 9 stage). Its `contract.ts` checks every citation against `docs/research/newfoundland-and-labrador/outcomes.json` (record: `gradelings-research/newfoundland-and-labrador/README.md`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`, which also writes `src/content/newfoundland-and-labrador/overall.ts`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), each citing a numbered category of Navigating the Early Years: An Early Childhood Learning Framework (2019) (`3.1` Investigation and Thinking; `src/content/newfoundland-and-labrador/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/newfoundland-and-labrador/prek/outcomes.json` (PDF in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Grade placement is provisional.** The grade-by-grade guides are not public, so math and language use the units Nova Scotia and New Brunswick both teach in that grade, science the units either teaches, and social studies general units plus the Newfoundland and Labrador-only ones. Replace the citations and placement when the guides can be read.
- **Report card:** Kindergarten to Grade 6 use the four-point rating (4 Excellent, 3 Good, 2 Approaching, 1 Not Yet Achieving). Grades 7 to 9 report percentage marks, so the framework uses the generic practice steps there. Same kid labels and "practice, not a report-card mark" rule.
- **Content:** BC units via `share`, other provinces' units via the plan in `plan.ts` (only where the questions are free of another province's names; its `contract.ts` checks), and Newfoundland and Labrador-only units in `src/content/newfoundland-and-labrador/units/` (`nl-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`). Helpers are in `kit.ts`.
- **Parent guides:** French, the Essential Graduation Learnings (`/guides/newfoundland-and-labrador/graduation-learnings/`) and the Provincial Reading and Mathematics Assessments (Grades 3 and 6; the schedule changes, so the guide points to the department).
- **Innu, Inuit, Southern Inuit and Mi'kmaq content** is deliberately light, in the present tense, and needs partner review before launch. Don't add more without that.
