# Ohio
- **Framework `us-oh`, slug `ohio`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are Ohio's Learning Standards (Ohio Department of Education and Workforce): mathematics (2017; `4.NBT.1`, Grade 9 cites the high school codes Ohio writes with a dot, `A.SSE.1`), English language arts (the 2026 K-8 standards, `RL.4.1`, `RF.4.3`, writing foundations `WF.4.3`; Grade 9 cites the 2017 9-10 standards, `RL.9-10.1`), science (2018; content statements `4.PS.2`, `6.LS.1`; Grade 9 cites the high school courses, `PS.M.1`, `B.DI.1`) and social studies (K-8 updated July 2026, numbered content statements without codes, cited "Statement 6"; Grade 9 the 2018 high school courses, "American Government 16", "Economics and Financial Literacy 3"). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`ohi()` in `src/content/ohio/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/ohio/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `gradelings-research/ohio/README.md`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), cited by Ohio's Early Learning and Development Standards' progression steps with the domain (`Math 1.c.4`) (`src/content/ohio/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/ohio/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** math and English language arts follow California's placement with Ohio codes (`src/content/ohio/plan/`); science follows Ohio's own grade placement (Earth and space, physical and life science topics per grade), copying units from whichever grade teaches the topic. Social studies follows Ohio's grade themes: a child's place in time and space (K, with Ohio's symbols), families now and long ago (1), interactions through time (2, with Ohio's regions), communities past and present (3, with Ohio's land, communities and local government), **Ohio in the United States (4, all Ohio's own)**, the Western Hemisphere (5), the Eastern Hemisphere (6), world studies from 750 BC to 1600 (7) and US studies from 1492 to 1877 (8); Grade 9 is American Government and Economics and Financial Literacy, both required for graduation. Ohio-only units (`oh-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) are in `src/content/ohio/units/`, plus filtered `oh-` copies (`localCopy` in `units/copies-g*.ts`) of other frameworks' units with Ohio examples. No unit Ohio uses may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`) outside US history and civics, and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`ohio.test.ts`). Native content (the Adena and Hopewell earthworks; the Shawnee, Lenape, Wyandot, Miami and Seneca of the Ohio Country, whose nations live on today mostly outside Ohio) is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; removal is told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are Ohio's State Tests (English language arts and math in grades 3 to 8, science in 5 and 8; five levels, Limited to Advanced), end-of-course exams in high school and the Kindergarten Readiness Assessment; the Third Grade Reading Guarantee (a parent decides on retention since 2023) is on the state tests guide.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/ohio/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, Ohio's K-12 Social and Emotional Learning Standards (five competencies, adopted 2019, not tested or graded; `/guides/ohio/sel-standards/`) and the state tests (`/guides/ohio/state-tests/`; dates point to the Department). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Ohio Revised Code 3319.325 to 3319.327 (Senate Bill 29) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Ohio.** `/privacy/` states our commitments under Ohio Revised Code sections 3319.325 to 3319.327 (Senate Bill 29, in force October 2024): student records we hold for a school belong to the school; we protect them as Chapter 1347 asks of a school district; we never sell or share them, never use them for marketing or advertising and never use them beyond the school's educational purposes; only staff who need them for their work can see them; we never turn on location tracking, a microphone or a camera, or monitor a student's activity outside the app; we give the school what it needs to list us in its yearly notice to families; we tell the school if a breach affects its students; and we delete or return the records within 90 days after our agreement with the school ends, or sooner when the school asks.

## Known gaps

- Ohio: Native content is light and needs partner review before it grows, and the 2026 K-8 social studies standards take effect in 2027-28, so check them against the Department's final edition then (see `gradelings-research/ohio/README.md`).
