# Northwest Territories
- **Framework `ca-nt`, slug `northwest-territories`**, Junior Kindergarten and Kindergarten to Grade 9, including BC's two French subjects. The NWT is replacing the Alberta curriculum with the BC curriculum adapted for the NWT, phased in since 2023 (NWT Education, Culture and Employment, JK-12 Curriculum Renewal; Grades 1 to 9 on it in 2026-27, Kindergarten to Grade 3 as a draft), reporting on BC's four-point proficiency scale. So an NWT course is the matching BC course with a `ca-nt` standards entry (`nwtGrade` in `src/content/nwt/kit.ts`); nothing is copied, and a new BC unit appears for the NWT automatically. Alberta is not used for the NWT.
- **Pre-K (Junior Kindergarten):** shown as "Junior Kindergarten" (`preKName`). It uses the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), each citing a numbered Integrated Curricular Outcome of the NWT Junior Kindergarten / Kindergarten Curriculum (2017), which keeps its own NWT curriculum while K to 12 moves to BC's (`11.4`; `src/content/nwt/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/northwest-territories/prek/outcomes.json` (PDF in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Dene Kede and Inuuqatigiit outcomes are not cited. Practice steps only.
- **NWT-only units** live in `src/content/nwt/units/` with `nt-` ids: Our Territory (Grade 3), Dene Kede (Grade 5) and Inuuqatigiit (Grade 6) in social studies. Dene and Inuvialuit content is light and in the present tense. Source and record: `docs/research/northwest-territories/`.
- **Guides, report-card page and wording** are BC's reworded for the NWT (`NORTHWEST_TERRITORIES` in `nwt/framework.ts`, `NWT_GUIDES` in `nwt/guides.ts`).
