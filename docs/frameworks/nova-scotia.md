# Nova Scotia
- **Framework `ca-ns`, slug `nova-scotia`**, Pre-primary and Primary (Kindergarten) to Grade 9 in math, language, science, social studies and both French subjects (Immersion Primary to Grade 9, Core French Grade 4 to 9). Standards are the learning outcomes of the Nova Scotia curriculum (curriculum.novascotia.ca). Math cites outcome codes (`N06`, `PR01`); Grade 9 language cites outcome numbers (`4.4`); language Primary to Grade 8 cites the strand or outcome title; science and social studies have no codes, so they cite the learning bundle or the outcome's headline. Its `contract.ts` checks every citation against `docs/research/nova-scotia/outcomes.json` (record: `gradelings-research/nova-scotia/README.md`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`). French cites course names and strands.
- **Pre-K (Pre-primary):** shown as "Pre-primary" (`preKName`). It uses the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), each citing a learning objective of *Capable, Confident, and Curious: Nova Scotia's Early Learning Curriculum Framework* as "Goal N" (`src/content/nova-scotia/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/nova-scotia/prek/outcomes.json` (PDF in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only: Pre-primary has no report card.
- **Report card:** the wording changes by grade band. Primary: Beginning, Developing, Meeting, Exceeding. Grades 1 to 3: ND, DE, WD. Grades 4 to 6: letter grades with Limited, Basic, Good, Thorough. Grades 7 to 9: percentage bands. Same kid labels and "practice, not a report-card mark" rule.
- **Content:** BC units via `share`, other provinces' units via `adopt(...)` (only where the questions are free of another province's names; its `contract.ts` checks), Nova Scotia-only units in `src/content/nova-scotia/` (`ns-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`, banks in `units/`). Each own bank needs at least 24 distinct items. Helpers are in `kit.ts`.
- **Parent guides:** French, Essential Graduation Competencies (`/guides/nova-scotia/graduation-competencies/`) and provincial assessments (Grades 3, 6 and 8; schedule changes, so the guide points to the department).
- **Mi'kmaw, Acadian, African Nova Scotian and Gaelic content** is deliberately light.
