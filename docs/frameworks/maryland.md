# Maryland
- **Framework `us-md`, slug `maryland`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are MSDE's Maryland College and Career Ready Standards and frameworks: mathematics (revised 2025, with new domains and codes, `4.NOS.A.1`, `3.GR.C.7`, `5.DS.A.1`; Grade 9 cites Algebra I and Geometry, `A.SSE.A.1`, `G.CO.A.1`), English language arts (2022, Common Core codes, `RL.4.1`; Grade 9 cites `RI.9-10.2`), science (NGSS codes; `MS-` under Grades 6 to 8, `HS-` under Grade 9) and social studies (Kindergarten to Grade 3 `C.1`, `G.2.b`, `E.3`, `H.1.d`; Grade 4 `WC`, `RC`, `AR`; Grade 5 `AG`, `CN`, `CA`; Grades 6 and 7 share the 2025 framework, `6/7.7.2`; Grade 8 `CL`, `FN`, `GE`, `CW`; Grade 9 American Government, `SOG.2`, `LB.1`, `JB.2`, `EP.3`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`mar()` in `src/content/maryland/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/maryland/outcomes.json` (the sources are the Common Standards Project's copies of MSDE's documents, in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `gradelings-research/maryland/README.md`).
- **Content:** math, English language arts and science follow California's placement with Maryland codes (`src/content/maryland/plan/`; each grade file's `POOL` lists the unit files it copies from); units for ideas the 2025 math revision moved to another grade are left out. California's science units that name California places are replaced by `md-` copies with Maryland examples (`units/science-g*.ts`), and other borrowed units that name another state's places by filtered copies (`localCopy`, `units/copies-g*.ts`). Social studies follows MSDE's grade frameworks: civics, geography, economics and history in Kindergarten to Grade 2 (Maryland's symbols in Grade 1), **Maryland in Grade 3 (its economy, the Chesapeake Bay and the land, local, state and national government, and the Native nations and early colonists; all Maryland's own units)**, colonial and revolutionary Maryland in Grade 4 (worlds collide, the Maryland colony, slavery in Maryland, the Revolution and a timeline), the Constitution and Maryland's place in ratification, the Civil War with Maryland as a border state, Reconstruction and Maryland's HBCUs, roads, rails and the telegraph, the free press and civil rights in Maryland in Grade 5, world history in Grades 6 and 7, United States history to Reconstruction with Maryland and the new nation and slavery and resistance in Grade 8, and American Government in Grade 9 (governing Maryland, the courts and landmark cases, the national government, elections and lawmaking, agencies and public policy, foreign policy and economic policy). Maryland-only units have `md-` ids (`bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/maryland/units/`. No borrowed unit may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`) outside US history and civics (Grades 4, 5, 8 and 9 social studies), and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`maryland.test.ts`). Piscataway, Accohannock and Pocomoke content is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; slavery and segregation in Maryland are told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale (each of the 24 school systems decides), so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are MCAP (English language arts and math in grades 3 to 8, science in 5 and 8, social studies in 8, and high school tests including government) and the Kindergarten Readiness Assessment; the Ready to Read Act's K-3 reading screening is on the report-card page.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/maryland/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, the learning practices built into the standards (`/guides/maryland/learning-practices/`; school systems choose their own SEL programs) and MCAP (`/guides/maryland/state-tests/`; dates point to MSDE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Maryland Student Data Privacy Act (Education Article, section 4-131) commitments next to the other states', with breach notice to the school without unreasonable delay.

## Schools

- **Maryland.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as an operator under Maryland's Student Data Privacy Act (Education Article, section 4-131): no targeted advertising, no sale or rental, no profile except for preschool to Grade 12 school purposes, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), reasonable security, and deletion within a reasonable time when the school or county board asks. We promise breach notice to the school without unreasonable delay, so it can notify families under the Maryland Personal Information Protection Act (Commercial Law, section 14-3504). Offer the district's own agreement or the NDPA if it asks. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- Maryland: English language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit; Grade 9 math is Algebra I only; the 2025 Grades 6-7 social studies framework's modern themes (industrialization to globalization, apartheid, the Middle East) and Grade 8's westward expansion and Jacksonian Democracy have no unit of their own; Piscataway, Accohannock and Pocomoke content is light and needs partner review (see `gradelings-research/maryland/README.md` and `gaps.txt`).
