# Arizona
- **Framework `us-az`, slug `arizona`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Arizona Academic Standards (Arizona Department of Education): 2016 math (codes with cluster letters, `4.NBT.A.1`; Grade 9 is Algebra 1, `A1.A-SSE.A.1`), 2016 ELA (`4.RL.1`; Grade 9 cites `9-10.RL.1`), the 2018 Arizona Science Standards (Arizona's own codes, `4.P4U1.1`; Grade 9 cites `HS.L2U1.20`) and the 2018 History and Social Science Standards (`4.H2.1`; Grade 9 cites `HS.C2.1`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`az()` in `src/content/arizona/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/arizona/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; azed.gov needs a real browser, so `fetch.sh` uses Playwright under `xvfb-run`; record: `gradelings-research/arizona/README.md`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), cited by the Arizona Early Learning Standards' indicators with the standard's name (`Math 1.4.a`) (`src/content/arizona/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/arizona/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** math and English language arts follow California's placement with Arizona codes (`src/content/arizona/plan/`); science follows Arizona's own placement (light and sound in Grade 3, electricity and magnets in 4, the solar system and tides in 6, cells and Newton's laws in 7), copying units from whichever grade teaches the topic. Social studies follows Arizona's grade themes, with **Arizona Studies in Grade 3** (all Arizona's own) and Integrated Global Studies in 7; Grade 9 is civics, with the Arizona Constitution, Arizona's government and the civics test. Arizona-only units (`az-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) are in `src/content/arizona/units/`, plus filtered `az-` copies (`copyOf`) of other states' units. No unit Arizona uses may name Washington State, Oregon, California or another state's own places as a place, or be built around another state, and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`arizona.test.ts`). Content about Arizona's 22 Tribes is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale (districts and charters choose; many use standards-based 1 to 4 reporting), so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are AASA (English language arts and math, grades 3 to 8), AzSCI (science, grades 5, 8 and 11) and ACT Aspire (grade 9).
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/arizona/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the eight Standards for Mathematical Practice (`/guides/arizona/math-practices/`, not graded on their own) and the state tests (`/guides/arizona/state-tests/`; dates point to ADE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Arizona Revised Statutes section 15-1046 (student data privacy) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Arizona.** `/privacy/` states our Arizona Revised Statutes section 15-1046 (student data privacy) commitments: no targeted advertising, no selling or renting, no profile beyond the school's purposes, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), a security program, and deletion within a reasonable time when the school asks. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.
