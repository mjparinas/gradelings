# Colorado
- **Framework `us-co`, slug `colorado`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Colorado Academic Standards (CDE): mathematics (2020; `4.NBT.A.1`, Grade 9 cites `HS.A-SSE.A.1`), reading, writing and communicating (2020; `4.2.1.a.ii`, Grade 9 cites the 9-10 band), science (2020; `PS1.4.1.a`, `LS2.MS.3.a`, `ESS3.HS.12.a`; no engineering codes, so design units cite the outcomes that ask for design) and social studies (2022; `4.H.1.a`, `4.C.2.e`, `4.PF.1.a`, Grade 9 cites `HS.C.2.j`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`col()` in `src/content/colorado/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/colorado/outcomes.json` (cde.state.co.us resets scripted connections, so the sources are the Common Standards Project's copies of CDE's documents; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `docs/research/colorado/README.md`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), each citing a preschool evidence outcome or indicator of the Colorado Academic Standards (`P.CC.B.2`, `PK.2.2.b`, `LS2.PK.1.f`, `PK.C.1.c`; `src/content/colorado/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/colorado/prek/outcomes.json` (the Common Standards Project's copies in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** math, English language arts and science follow California's placement with Colorado codes (`src/content/colorado/plan/`), copying the same units from the same files (each grade file's `POOL`); California's science units that name California places are replaced by `co-` copies with Colorado examples, and other borrowed units that name another state's places by filtered copies (`localCopy`). Social studies follows Colorado's grade focus: community and region (K to 3, with Colorado's symbols in 1 and its land, communities and local government in 3), **Colorado history and geography in Grade 4 (all Colorado's own)**, early North America to the founding (5), the Western Hemisphere (6) and Eastern Hemisphere (7), mostly New York's units, United States history from the Revolution through Reconstruction with the nation's move west (8) and civics (9), with Colorado-only units (`co-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/colorado/units/`. No borrowed unit may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`) outside US history and civics, and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`colorado.test.ts`). Ute, Cheyenne, Arapaho and Ancestral Puebloan content is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; the Sand Creek Massacre, the removal of the Ute people and Japanese American incarceration at Amache are told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are CMAS (English language arts and math in grades 3 to 8, science in 5, 8 and 11, social studies in 4 or 7 at a sample of schools), the PSAT and SAT in high school and CoAlt; the READ Act's K-3 reading checks are on the state tests guide.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/colorado/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the Colorado Essential Skills (four groups, not graded; `/guides/colorado/essential-skills/`) and the state tests (`/guides/colorado/state-tests/`; dates point to CDE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Student Data Transparency and Security Act (C.R.S. 22-16-101 to 22-16-112) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Colorado.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as a provider under Colorado's Student Data Transparency and Security Act (C.R.S. 22-16-101 to 22-16-112): we say what student information we collect and why, use it only for the school's educational purposes, never sell it, never use it for targeted advertising, never build a profile beyond those purposes, bind our listed providers to the same limits by contract, keep a security program in place, tell the school about a breach, and delete the information when the school asks or the agreement ends. Colorado districts publish the providers they use and may sign a contract that adds terms; offer the district's own agreement or the NDPA with Colorado's exhibit if it asks. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- Colorado: Ute, Cheyenne and Arapaho content is light and needs partner review before it grows, and the Holocaust and genocide standards and most high school history and geography expectations have no unit of their own (see `docs/research/colorado/README.md`).
