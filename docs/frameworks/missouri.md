# Missouri
- **Framework `us-mo`, slug `missouri`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Missouri Learning Standards' Grade-Level Expectations (DESE, adopted by the State Board in April 2016): math (`4.NBT.A.1`; Grade 9 cites Algebra 1, `A1.LQE.A.3`, and Geometry, `G.CO.A.1`), English language arts (`4.R.1.A.a`, Grades 6 to 8 `6.RL.1.A`; Grade 9 cites the 9-10 band, `9-10.RL.1.A`), science (`4.PS3.A.1`; middle school is one band, `6-8.LS1.A.2`, valid in Grades 6, 7 and 8; Grade 9 cites `9-12.LS2.A.1`) and social studies (`4.H.3.B.a`; middle school courses share one band, `6-8.AH.5.CC.C`, `6-8.WH.3.CC.A`, `6-8.GEO.2.PC.A`; Grade 9 cites high school Government, `9-12.GV.3.GS.C`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`mos()` in `src/content/missouri/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/missouri/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; some teacher-view codes have no text and are listed in `gradelings-research/missouri/README.md`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), cited by the Missouri Early Learning Standards' indicators with the content area (`Mathematics I.A.2`; the standards have no social studies area, so social units cite social-emotional and physical development indicators) (`src/content/missouri/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/missouri/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** math and English language arts follow California's placement with Missouri codes (`src/content/missouri/plan/`); science has Missouri's own Kindergarten to Grade 5 placement (forces and motion in 4, light in 5) and follows California's integrated middle school, copying units from whichever grade teaches the topic (each grade file's `POOL`). California's and other states' science units built around their own places are replaced by `mo-` copies with Missouri examples (`units/science-g*.ts`). Social studies: from the child to the community and nation in Kindergarten to Grade 2 (American symbols in 1, Missouri's regions in 2), **Missouri studies in Grade 3 (all Missouri's own units)**, United States history before 1800 (4) and after it (5), world geography (6), world history to about 1450 (7), American history to about 1870 with Missouri as the crossroads of the nation (8) and Government (9), with the US and Missouri Constitutions course and the civics test Missouri requires for graduation (RSMo 170.011, 170.345). Missouri-only units (`mo-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) are in `src/content/missouri/units/`, plus filtered `mo-` copies (`localCopy`). No unit Missouri uses may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`) outside United States history and civics (Grades 4, 5, 8 and 9), and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`missouri.test.ts`). Osage, Missouria and Mississippian content is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; slavery, the Missouri Compromise, Dred Scott, removal and the border war are told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are the Missouri Assessment Program (MAP: Grade-Level Assessments in English language arts and math in grades 3 to 8 and science in 5 and 8, End-of-Course assessments in high school, four achievement levels from Below Basic to Advanced); reading success plans and the 3rd grade retention talk (RSMo 167.645) are on the state tests guide.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/missouri/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the four performance goals of the Show-Me Standards (`/guides/missouri/show-me-standards/`; not graded on their own; Missouri has no statewide SEL standards) and the MAP (`/guides/missouri/state-tests/`; dates point to DESE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** Missouri has no student online privacy statute for technology providers (RSMo 161.096 covers the state's own data system), so `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` give Missouri schools the same commitments as the other states', with breach notice to the school without delay under RSMo 407.1500.

## Schools

- **Missouri.** Missouri has no student online privacy statute binding technology providers (section 161.096, RSMo, governs the state's own student data system and what DESE may collect), so `/privacy/` gives Missouri schools the same commitments as the other states', under the school's agreement and FERPA and COPPA. Missouri's breach notification law (section 407.1500, RSMo) asks anyone who maintains personal information for another to notify the owner immediately after discovering a breach; we tell the school without delay. Offer the district's own agreement or the NDPA if it asks (many Missouri districts sign it through the Missouri Student Privacy Alliance). See `gradelings-research/missouri/README.md`.

## Known gaps

- Missouri: Osage, Missouria and Mississippian content is light and needs partner review before it grows; 2.PS2.A.1 and the high school heredity and evolution expectations (9-12.LS3.B.1, LS3.B.2, LS4.A.2) have no unit; some social studies expectations have no text in DESE's teacher views and are uncited (see `gradelings-research/missouri/README.md`).
