# Pennsylvania
- **Framework `us-pa`, slug `pennsylvania`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Pennsylvania Department of Education's, as pdesas.org publishes them: the PA Core Standards for math (`CC.2.1.4.B.1`; Grade 9 is Algebra I, the `CC.2.*.HS.*` strands) and English language arts (`CC.1.2.4.A`; Grade 9 cites the 9-10 band), the STEELS science standards (`3.1.4.A`, with K-2, 3-5, 6-8 and 9-12 bands) and the civics (`5.1.4.A`), economics (`6.1.3-5.A`), geography (`7.2.5.B`), history (`8.3.4.B`) and personal finance (`17.1.K-2.A`) standards, which repeat at every grade. Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`pa()` in `src/content/pennsylvania/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/pennsylvania/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record and the code formats: `docs/research/pennsylvania/README.md`; verified state facts: `FACTS.md`). The social studies documents are PDE's voluntary working revisions (2009 to 2012), not the Chapter 4 text, and the economics file is the proposed rule, so parent copy says "PDE's academic standards".
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), cited by the Pennsylvania Learning Standards for Early Childhood, Pre-Kindergarten (2024) by their own codes (`2.1 PK.A.2.1`); pre-K math has no pattern standard, so patterns cites sorting and ordering by attribute (`src/content/pennsylvania/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/pennsylvania/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`src/content/pennsylvania/plan/`, pools in `pools/<subject>-<grade>.ts`, `pennsylvaniaGrade` in `kit.ts`), plus Pennsylvania-only units (`pa-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/pennsylvania/units/`, with lessons in `src/content/lessons/`. Science puts Earth and space in Grade 6, life science and the environment in 7 and physical science and engineering in 8; Grade 9 is Algebra I, biology and physical science, and civics. Social studies is communities (Kindergarten to Grade 2), Pennsylvania communities and local government (3), **Pennsylvania studies (4, mostly Pennsylvania's own units)**, early US history (5), world cultures (6), US history and government (7), US history and civics with Pennsylvania (8) and civics and government with the US and Pennsylvania Constitutions (9). No unit Pennsylvania borrows may name another state's places outside US history and civics (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`; `copyOf` makes filtered `pa-` copies), and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`pennsylvania.test.ts`). No federally recognized tribe resides in Pennsylvania, so Lenape, Susquehannock, Shawnee, Seneca and other nations' content is light, in the present tense, names the nations whose descendants live elsewhere, and needs partner review before it grows.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale (each district decides), so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are the PSSA (English language arts and math in grades 3 to 8, science in grades 5 and 8) and the Keystone Exams; the guide points to PDE for dates.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/pennsylvania/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the Pennsylvania Career Ready Skills (`/guides/pennsylvania/career-ready-skills/`; optional SEL progressions, not graded) and the state tests (`/guides/pennsylvania/state-tests/`; says a religious-belief excusal is the only one the regulation allows). No French guide.
- **Schools:** Pennsylvania has no student online privacy statute for technology providers, so `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` give Pennsylvania schools the same commitments as the other states', and say we tell a school without delay if we discover a breach of data we hold for it (Act 151 of 2022 sets no number of days for a vendor). ## Schools

- **Pennsylvania.** Pennsylvania has no student online privacy statute binding technology providers (Senate Bill 378 was laid on the table on June 3, 2026), so `/privacy/` gives Pennsylvania schools the same commitments as the other states', under the school's agreement and FERPA and COPPA. Pennsylvania's breach notification laws apply: under Act 151 of 2022 a vendor that holds a public school's data must tell the school "following discovery" of a breach (the statute sets no number of days; the seven-business-day deadline binds the school, not a vendor), and Act 33 of 2024 adds notice to the Attorney General when more than 500 residents are affected. Many districts sign the Student Data Privacy Consortium's agreement through the Pennsylvania Student Privacy Alliance; offer the district's own agreement or the NDPA if it asks. See `docs/research/pennsylvania/FACTS.md`.

## Known gaps

- Pennsylvania: English language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency, and the STEELS technology and engineering standards that need building or free text, have no unit; Grade 9 math is Algebra I only (geometry, complex numbers and trigonometry are left out); social studies covers Pennsylvania history after about 1970 and the detailed Grade 9 economics and personal finance codes only lightly; the economics standards file is the proposed rule and the civics, geography and history documents are PDE's voluntary working revisions, so recheck both against the final text; Lenape, Susquehannock, Shawnee and Seneca content is light and needs partner review (see `docs/research/pennsylvania/README.md` and its `gaps-*.txt`).
