# Tennessee
- **Framework `us-tn`, slug `tennessee`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Tennessee Academic Standards (State Board of Education): math (2021; `4.NBT.A.1`, with the cluster letter; Grade 9 is Algebra I, `A1.A.REI.B.2`), English language arts (2019; `4.RL.KID.1`, foundational literacy `4.FL.PWR.3`, Grades 6 to 8 `6.L.CSE.1`; Grade 9 cites the 9-10 band, `9-10.RL.KID.1`), science (2022; `4.PS3.1`, `6.ESS2.4`; Grade 9 cites Biology I, `BIO1.LS1.1`, and Physical Science, `PSCI.PS2.1`) and social studies (2024; `4.36`, `K.05`; Grade 9 cites United States Government and Civics, `GC.16`, and Economics, `E.09`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`tns()` in `src/content/tennessee/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/tennessee/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; tn.gov refuses scripts, so `fetch.sh` uses a headed Playwright browser under `xvfb-run`; rebuild with `build_outcomes.py`; record: `gradelings-research/tennessee/README.md`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), cited by the Tennessee Early Learning Developmental Standards for four-year-olds by their own codes (`PK.CC.B.5.a`); the 2018 standards have no pattern standard, so patterns cites sorting by attributes (`src/content/tennessee/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/tennessee/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** math and English language arts follow California's placement with Tennessee codes (`src/content/tennessee/plan/`); science follows Tennessee's own grade placement, copying units from whichever grade teaches the topic (each grade file's `POOL`). Social studies follows the 2024 standards: self and symbols (K), Tennessee's map, symbols and leaders (1), its neighbor states, constitutions and monuments (2), United States history to westward expansion with the Tennessee Volunteers (3), the Civil War to civil rights (4), **Tennessee history (5, mostly Tennessee's own units)**, the ancient world (6), the Middle Ages to exploration (7), United States history to Reconstruction with Jacksonian and divided Tennessee (8), and Government and Civics with Economics (9). Tennessee-only units (`tn-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) are in `src/content/tennessee/units/`, plus filtered `tn-` copies (`localCopy` in `units/copies-g*.ts`, `units/math-g9.ts`) of other frameworks' units with Tennessee examples. No unit Tennessee uses may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`) outside US history and civics, and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`tennessee.test.ts`). Cherokee, Chickasaw, Shawnee and Creek content is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; removal, the Trail of Tears and the loss of free Black men's vote in 1834 are told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale for Kindergarten to Grade 8 (high school uses the uniform grading scale), so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are TCAP (English language arts, math and science in grades 3 to 8, social studies in grades 6 to 8, end-of-course tests in high school); the 3rd grade promotion law (T.C.A. 49-6-3115) is on the state tests guide.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/tennessee/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the eight Standards for Mathematical Practice (`/guides/tennessee/math-practices/`; Tennessee has no statewide SEL standards for students) and TCAP (`/guides/tennessee/state-tests/`; dates point to the Department). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Student Online Personal Protection Act (Tennessee Code Annotated 49-1-708) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Tennessee.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as an operator under Tennessee's Student Online Personal Protection Act (Public Chapter 757 of 2016, Tennessee Code Annotated section 49-1-708, in the Data Accessibility, Transparency and Accountability Act): no targeted advertising, no profile beyond the school's purposes, no sale or rental, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), reasonable security, and deletion within a reasonable time when the school asks. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- Tennessee: English language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit; Grade 9 is Algebra I, Biology I and Physical Science only; Cherokee, Chickasaw, Shawnee and Creek content is light and needs partner review; the 2024 social studies standards reach classrooms in 2027-28, so recheck them then (see `gradelings-research/tennessee/README.md` and `gaps.txt`).
