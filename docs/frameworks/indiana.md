# Indiana
- **Framework `us-in`, slug `indiana`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English/language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Indiana Academic Standards (IDOE, 2023; revised math and social studies start in 2027-28): mathematics (`4.NS.1`, the Process Standards `PS.1` to `PS.8` in every grade; Grade 9 is Algebra I, `AI.L.1`, with a few Geometry standards, `G.T.1`), English/language arts (`4.RC.1`, `2.RF.3`; Grade 9 cites `9-10.RC.1`), science (NGSS-style codes placed by Indiana's grade documents, `4-PS3-1`, `MS-LS1-1`; Grade 9 cites Biology and Integrated Chemistry and Physics) and social studies (`4.H.13`, `3.C.2`; Grade 6 adds Middle School Civics, `6.CIV.1`; Grade 9 cites United States Government, `USG.3.2`, and Economics, `E.2.1`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`ind()` in `src/content/indiana/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/indiana/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record and gaps: `gradelings-research/indiana/README.md` and `gaps.txt`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), cited by the Indiana Early Learning Standards' older-preschool indicators (`M1.1.o.e`) (`src/content/indiana/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/indiana/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`src/content/indiana/plan/`; each grade file's `POOL` lists the unit files it copies from, `indianaGrade` in `kit.ts`), plus Indiana-only units (`in-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/indiana/units/`. Math and English/language arts follow California's placement where Indiana teaches the same idea in the same grade; science and social studies follow Indiana's own placement. California, Washington and Oregon science units that name their places are Indiana copies with Indiana examples (`units/science-g*.ts`), and other borrowed units that name another state's places are filtered copies (`localCopy`); `indiana.test.ts` checks no other borrowed unit names another state's places outside US history and civics (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`), and that no other state's Tribal units are borrowed.
- **Social studies** follows Indiana's grade themes: rules, family and community (K), my community long ago and today (1), communities and citizenship with the Pledge and Benjamin Harrison (2), Indiana communities and the Midwest (3), **Indiana studies (4, all Indiana's own units: land and regions, first peoples, territory and statehood, Lincoln and the Civil War, growth, government, economy and a timeline)**, the United States to the founding (5), Europe and the Americas with Middle School Civics and the Indiana Constitution (6), Africa, Asia and the Southwest Pacific (7), United States history to Reconstruction (8) and United States Government with Economics (9). Miami, Potawatomi, Delaware (Lenape) and Shawnee content is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; removal is told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale for Kindergarten to Grade 8, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are ILEARN (English/language arts and math in grades 3 to 8 with checkpoints during the year, science in 4 and 6, social studies in 5, Biology in high school) and IREAD (grades 2 and 3; the 3rd grade reading rule is on the state tests guide).
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/indiana/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the Process Standards for mathematics and the thinking skills in the other subjects (`/guides/indiana/process-standards/`, not graded on their own) and the state tests (`/guides/indiana/state-tests/`; dates point to IDOE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** Indiana has no student online privacy statute for technology providers, so `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` give Indiana schools the same commitments as the other states', with breach notice to the school without unreasonable delay and within 45 days (Indiana Code 24-4.9). Keep that true.

## Schools

- **Indiana.** Indiana has no student online privacy statute binding technology providers, so `/privacy/` gives Indiana schools the same commitments as the other states', under the school's agreement and FERPA and COPPA. Indiana's breach notification law (IC 24-4.9) has a business that keeps data for another tell its owner of a breach, and the owner notify residents without unreasonable delay and within 45 days; we promise the school notice without unreasonable delay and within 45 days at most. Offer the district's own agreement or the NDPA if it asks.

## Known gaps

- Indiana: English/language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit; Grade 9 math is Algebra I with a few Geometry standards; Grade 7 and 8 social studies and Grade 9 government and economics are covered only in part; Miami, Potawatomi, Delaware (Lenape) and Shawnee content is light and needs partner review (see `gradelings-research/indiana/README.md` and `gaps.txt`).
