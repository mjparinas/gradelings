# Oregon
- **Framework `us-or`, slug `oregon`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like Washington. Standards are ODE's: 2021 math (`4.NBT.A.1`, high school `HS.*` under Grade 9), 2026 ELA (`4.RL.1`, `ELA.9-10.*`), 2022 science with Oregon's dotted codes (`4.ESS2.1`, `MS.ETS1.x`) and 2024 social science (`4.H.1`; the 6/7 band `6/7.H.CP.11` counts in both grades). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`ore()` in `src/content/oregon/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/oregon/outcomes.json` (rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`).
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; Washington's and the provinces' units via `adopt` (Washington's grade is first in each pool), plus Oregon-only units (`or-` ids) in `src/content/oregon/units/`. Washington-only (`wa-`), Oregon-only (`or-`) and California-only (`ca-`) units may be adopted by other US states (California adopts the first two, New York all three where they name no other state, Maine all three outside science, Minnesota all three where they name no other state) but never by a Canadian framework. No unit Oregon uses may mention Washington State, and Washington's Tribal units are never borrowed; where a Washington unit fits except for a few items, Oregon has a filtered `or-` copy. Grade 4 (Oregon and its nine federally recognized Tribes, Tribal History/Shared History) and most of Grades 5 to 9 social studies are written for Oregon.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as Washington. State tests are OSAS.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Oregon Student Information Protection Act (ORS 336.184) commitments next to Washington's SUPER Act ones; keep them true when student data handling changes.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/oregon/guides.ts`, US English, registered in `GUIDES`): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, ODE's Transformative SEL standards (five domains, adopted June 2023, not graded; served at `/guides/oregon/sel-standards/`) and OSAS (`/guides/oregon/state-tests/`: ELA and math in grades 3 to 8 and 11, science in 5, 8 and 11, the ORS 329.479 opt-out for ELA and math; dates point to ODE). No French guide.

## Schools

- **Oregon law.** `/privacy/` states the matching commitments under the Oregon Student Information Protection Act (ORS 336.184): no targeted advertising, no sale, no profiling beyond the school's purpose, sharing only as the law allows, a security program and deletion when the school asks.
