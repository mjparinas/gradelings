# Yukon
- **Framework `ca-yt`, slug `yukon`**, Early Kindergarten and Kindergarten to Grade 9, including BC's two French subjects. Yukon implements the BC curriculum adapted for the Yukon and reports on BC's Provincial Proficiency Scale, so a Yukon course is the matching BC course with a `ca-yt` standards entry (`yukonGrade` in `src/content/yukon/kit.ts`); nothing is copied, and a new BC unit appears for Yukon automatically.
- **Pre-K (Early Kindergarten):** shown as "Early Kindergarten" (`preKName`), the optional K4 year in rural Yukon schools. Its Early Kindergarten Policy (2021) says it follows the BC Kindergarten curriculum, informed by the BC Early Learning Framework, so the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`) cite the BC Early Learning Framework exactly as BC does (`src/content/yukon/pk.ts`), checked against BC's `docs/research/bc/prek/outcomes.json`; the policy is in `docs/research/yukon/prek/`. Practice steps only.
- **Yukon-only units** (Yukon First Nations governance and citizenship in Grade 5 social studies) live in `src/content/yukon/units/` with `yk-` ids. Add other Yukon additions the same way. Yukon First Nations content is light and in the present tense. Source and record: `docs/research/yukon/`.
- **Guides, report-card page and wording** are BC's reworded for the Yukon (`YUKON` in `yukon/framework.ts`, `YUKON_GUIDES` in `yukon/guides.ts`).
