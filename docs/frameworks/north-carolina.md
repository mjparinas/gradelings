# North Carolina
- **Framework `us-nc`, slug `north-carolina`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the North Carolina Standard Course of Study (NCDPI): the 2023 mathematics standards (`NC.4.NBT.5`; Grade 9 is NC Math 1, `NC.M1.A-SSE.1`), the 2017 English language arts standards (`RL.4.1`, `RF.2.3`; Grade 9 cites the 9-10 band; the 2026 standards, in schools from 2027-28, are in `source/` but not cited yet), the 2023 science standards (`PS.4.1.2`, `LS.7.2.1`, `ESS.6.3.1`; Grade 9 cites Biology, `LS.Bio.1.1`, and Physical Science, `PS.PSc.2.1`) and the 2021 social studies standards (`4.H.1.2`, `4.C&G.1.3`; Grade 9 cites Civic Literacy, `CL.C&G.2.1`, and Economics and Personal Finance, `EPF.MCM.1.1`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`nca()` in `src/content/north-carolina/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/north-carolina/outcomes.json` (each document is a public Google Doc exported by `fetch.sh`; `build_outcomes.py` rebuilds the JSON; record and gaps: `docs/research/north-carolina/README.md` and `gaps.txt`).
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`src/content/north-carolina/plan/`; each grade file's `POOL` lists the unit files it copies from), plus North Carolina-only units (`nc-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/north-carolina/units/`. Math and English language arts follow Illinois's placement with North Carolina codes; science and social studies follow North Carolina's own grade placement. Borrowed units that name another state's places, Canada or Tribes are filtered North Carolina copies (`localCopy`, `units/copies-g*.ts`; `copyOf` now also drops questions with `canadianWords`); `north-carolina.test.ts` checks no other borrowed unit names another state's places outside US history and civics, and that no other state's Tribal units are borrowed.
- **Social studies** follows the grade themes: cultures, rules and needs (K), communities (1), America: our nation (2), **North Carolina communities (3)**, **North Carolina studies (4, all North Carolina's own units)**, United States history (5), world history to about 1400 (6) and since (7), **North Carolina and United States history (8, mostly North Carolina's own units)** and Civic Literacy with Economics and Personal Finance (9), both graduation requirements. American Indian content (the eight state-recognized tribes, among them the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians and the Lumbee Tribe) is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; the Lumbee's federal status is not stated. Slavery, the Wilmington coup of 1898, Jim Crow and the Greensboro sit-ins are told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are the End-of-Grade tests (reading and math in grades 3 to 8, science in 5 and 8), End-of-Course tests (NC Math 1, NC Math 3, English II, Biology), Read to Achieve with mCLASS checks in Kindergarten to Grade 3, the Kindergarten Entry Assessment and NCEXTEND1.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/north-carolina/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, the Portrait of a Graduate's seven durable skills (`/guides/north-carolina/portrait-of-a-graduate/`, not graded; North Carolina has no statewide SEL standards) and the state tests (`/guides/north-carolina/state-tests/`; dates point to NCDPI). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our G.S. 115C-401.2 (student data protection by operators) commitments next to the other states', including deletion within 45 days when a school asks.

## Schools

- **North Carolina.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as an operator under North Carolina's law on the protection of student data (G.S. 115C-401.2, 2015): no targeted advertising, no sale or rental, no profile beyond the school's K-12 purposes, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), reasonable security, breach notice to the school without unreasonable delay (North Carolina's Identity Theft Protection Act, G.S. 75-65, asks for notice without unreasonable delay), and deletion within 45 days when the school or district asks or tells us it has stopped using the service. Deleting a student in the teacher screens removes the data at once, which keeps the 45 days true. The statute is in `docs/research/north-carolina/source/law/`. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- North Carolina: English language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit, nor do a few math standards (NC Math 1 coordinate geometry among them); Grade 9 science covers Biology and Physical Science only; behavioral sciences and some economics and geography objectives are covered lightly; the 2026 English language arts standards are not cited yet; American Indian content is light and needs partner review (see `docs/research/north-carolina/README.md` and `gaps.txt`).
