# Virginia
- **Framework `us-va`, slug `virginia`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Virginia Standards of Learning (VDOE): 2023 mathematics (`4.NS.3`, strands NS, CE, MG, PS and PFA; Grade 9 is Algebra 1, `A.EO.2`, `A.F.1`), 2024 English (`4.RL.2`, `1.FFR.3`, `5.LU.2`, `6.C.4`), 2018 science (`4.6`; Life Science `LS.5` in Grade 7, Physical Science `PS.8` in 8, Earth Science `ES.7` and Biology `BIO.3` in 9) and 2023 history and social science (`2.7`; then Virginia Studies `VS.5` in Grade 4, United States History to 1865 `USI.6` in 5, 1865 to the Present `USII.4` in 6, Civics and Economics `CE.3` in 7, World Geography `WG.12` in 8 and World History and Geography to 1500 `WHI.5` in 9). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`va()` in `src/content/virginia/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/virginia/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; doe.virginia.gov blocks scripts, so `fetch.sh` uses headed Playwright under `xvfb-run`; `build_outcomes.py` rebuilds the JSON; record and gaps: `docs/research/virginia/README.md` and `gaps.txt`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), cited by Virginia's Early Learning and Development Standards' Later Preschool indicators (`CD3.1w`) (`src/content/virginia/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/virginia/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`src/content/virginia/plan/`, `virginiaGrade` in `kit.ts`; each grade file's `POOL` lists the unit files it copies from), plus Virginia-only units (`va-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/virginia/units/`. Borrowed units that name another state's places are filtered Virginia copies (`localCopy`, `units/copies.ts`); `virginia.test.ts` checks no other borrowed unit names another state's places outside US history and civics (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`, which lets George Washington and Washington, D.C. through), and that no other state's Tribal units are borrowed.
- **Social studies** follows the 2023 sequence: Virginia's holidays, symbols and early history (K and 1), Indigenous peoples, explorers, founders and changemakers (2), ancient Egypt, China, Greece, Rome and **Mali** (3), **Virginia Studies (4, Virginia's own units: regions, first peoples, Jamestown, colonial Virginia, the Revolution, the new nation, the Civil War, Reconstruction, modern Virginia and Virginia today)**, US history to 1865 (5) and since 1865 (6), civics and economics with Virginia's government (7), world geography (8, Canada left out) and world history to 1500 (9). Content about Virginia's eleven state-recognized tribes is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; slavery, Massive Resistance and segregation are told plainly.
- **Report card:** school divisions choose their report cards, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are the SOL tests (reading and math in grades 3 to 8, science in 5 and 8, writing in 8, with fall and winter growth tests in grades 3 to 8), the Virginia Literacy Act's K-3 reading screener and the Virginia Kindergarten Readiness Program.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/virginia/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the 5 C's of the Profile of a Virginia Graduate (`/guides/virginia/5-cs/`, not graded) and the SOL tests (`/guides/virginia/state-tests/`; dates point to VDOE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our commitments as a school service provider under the Code of Virginia, section 22.1-289.01, next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Virginia.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as a school service provider under the Code of Virginia, section 22.1-289.01: we use student personal information only for the school's educational purposes, never for targeted advertising, never sell it, never build a profile beyond those purposes, keep a security program in place, delete it when the school asks (within a reasonable time), say clearly what we collect and how we use it, and tell the school about a breach. Section 22.1-287.02 has the Department of Education and each school division keep their own data security policies, and many divisions sign the Virginia Student Data Privacy Consortium's agreement (the NDPA with Virginia's exhibit); offer the division's own agreement or that one if it asks. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- Virginia: English standards that need writing, speaking, research or fluency have no unit; a few math standards, `USII.9`, `WG.2` and most Grade 9 Earth Science standards are not practised yet; content about Virginia's tribes is light and needs partner review (see `docs/research/virginia/README.md` and `gaps.txt`).
