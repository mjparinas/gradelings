# Georgia
- **Framework `us-ga`, slug `georgia`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Georgia Department of Education's (GaDOE), from its CASE standards server (case.georgiastandards.org; the georgiastandards.org pages were retired in 2025): the K-12 Mathematics Standards (2021; `4.NR.2.3`, big ideas NR, PAR, MDR, GSR, PR, FGR and DSR; Grade 9 cites Algebra: Concepts and Connections, `A.FGR.2.2`, and parts of Geometry: Concepts and Connections, `G.PAR.2.3`), the K-12 English Language Arts Standards (in schools from 2025-26; `4.T.T.1`, domains Foundations, Language, Texts and Practices), the Georgia Standards of Excellence for science (2016; `S4P3.c`, Kindergarten `SKE1.a`; middle school by domain, Earth in 6, life in 7, physical in 8; Grade 9 cites Biology, `SB1.a`, and Physical Science, `SPS7.a`) and for social studies (`SS4H1.a`, `SS8CG2.c`; Grade 9 cites American Government/Civics, `SSCG17.a`, and Personal Finance and Economics, `SSPFL5.c`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`geo()` in `src/content/georgia/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/georgia/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record and gaps: `docs/research/georgia/README.md` and `gaps.txt`).
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`src/content/georgia/plan/`; each grade file's `POOL` lists the unit files it copies from), plus Georgia-only units (`ga-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/georgia/units/`. Math and English language arts follow California's placement where Georgia teaches the same idea in the same grade; science and social studies follow Georgia's own grade placement. Borrowed units that name another state's places are filtered Georgia copies (`localCopy`, `units/copies-g*.ts`); `georgia.test.ts` checks no other borrowed unit names another state's places outside US history and civics (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`, which lets "Spanish Florida" through), and that no other state's Tribal units are borrowed.
- **Social studies** follows the GSE grade themes: holidays, symbols and good citizens (K), American heroes (1), **Georgia's history, regions, leaders and the Muscogee (Creek) and Cherokee (2)**, American Indian cultural regions, explorers, colonies and the three branches (3), US history from the Revolution to Reconstruction (4), US history from 1900 to today (5), Latin America, Europe and Australia (6), Africa, Southwest Asia and Southern and Eastern Asia (7), **Georgia Studies (8, all Georgia's own units; Georgia Milestones tests it)** and American Government/Civics with Personal Finance and Economics (9). Canada in Grade 6 (`SS6G4` to `SS6G6`, `SS6H2`) is left out on purpose. Muscogee (Creek) and Cherokee content is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; removal, slavery, Jim Crow and the 1906 Atlanta Race Massacre are told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale for Kindergarten to Grade 8, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are Georgia Milestones (End-of-Grade: English language arts and math in grades 3 to 8, science in 5 and 8, social studies in 8; End-of-Course in high school), the Georgia Early Literacy Act's K-3 reading screeners, and GKIDS in kindergarten; the promotion rules (grade 3 reading, grades 5 and 8 reading and math) are on the state tests guide.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/georgia/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, the learning practices (`/guides/georgia/learning-practices/`: the Mathematical Practices, literacy, science and social studies skills; Georgia has no statewide SEL standards) and Georgia Milestones (`/guides/georgia/state-tests/`; dates point to GaDOE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Student Data Privacy, Accessibility, and Transparency Act (O.C.G.A. 20-2-660 to 20-2-668) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Georgia.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as an operator under Georgia's Student Data Privacy, Accessibility, and Transparency Act (O.C.G.A. sections 20-2-660 to 20-2-668, 2015): no targeted advertising, no sale or rental, no profile beyond the school's purposes, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), reasonable security, breach notice to the school without unreasonable delay (Georgia's breach law, O.C.G.A. 10-1-912, asks for the most expedient time possible), and deletion when the school asks. The Act also has the Georgia Department of Education and each district keep a data inventory and publish their privacy policies; offer the district's own agreement or the NDPA with Georgia's exhibit if it asks. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- Georgia: English language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit; Grade 6 and 7 regional economics and government are covered lightly and Canada in Grade 6 is left out; Grade 9 science covers Biology and Physical Science only; Muscogee (Creek) and Cherokee content is light and needs partner review (see `docs/research/georgia/README.md` and `gaps.txt`).
