# Prince Edward Island
- **Framework `ca-pe`, slug `prince-edward-island`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, language, science, social studies and Core French (Grades 4 to 9). **No French Immersion**: its curriculum guides are not in the repository. Standards are the specific curriculum outcomes in the Department of Education and Lifelong Learning's *Specific Curriculum Outcomes Framework* (Kindergarten to Grade 6, 2013), its Grade 9 mathematics and social studies guides and the Intermediate Program of Studies, cited by code (`N1; N3`, `4.3`, `4.2.1`; science cites the unit title). Its `contract.ts` checks every citation against `docs/research/prince-edward-island/outcomes.json` (record: `gradelings-research/prince-edward-island/README.md` and `EXTRACTION_REPORT.md`; the site blocks automated downloads, so a person runs `fetch.sh` locally and commits the PDFs in `source/`).
- **Grades 7 and 8 are placeholders:** their guides are not in the repository, so math cites strands, English language arts the general curriculum outcomes, science the unit names, social studies the course themes and Core French one placeholder. Replace them when the guides are added.
- **Report card:** no Prince Edward Island scale could be confirmed, so the framework uses four plain practice steps (Beginning, Approaching, Meeting, Exceeding), like Alberta and Nunavut. Same kid labels and "practice, not a report-card mark" rule.
- **Content:** BC units via `share`, other provinces' units via the plan in `plan.ts` (only where the questions are free of another province's names), and Prince Edward Island-only units in `src/content/prince-edward-island/units/` (`pe-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`), mostly science and social studies, which are specific to the province. Partial gaps in math, English language arts and Core French have no unit yet.
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), each citing a learning goal of the PEI Early Learning Framework (`Problem Solving and Numeracy`, `Language and Literacies`, `Curious Investigation`; Prince Edward Island Department of Education and Early Childhood Development) (`src/content/prince-edward-island/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/prince-edward-island/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py` in gradelings-research). Practice steps only.
- **Parent guides:** French, the CAMET competencies (`/guides/prince-edward-island/camet-competencies/`) and the provincial literacy and mathematics assessments (Grades 3 and 6; the schedule changes, so the guide points to the department).
- **Mi'kmaq content** is deliberately light, in the present tense, and needs partner review before launch. Don't add more without that.
