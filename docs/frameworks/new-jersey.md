# New Jersey

## Schools

- **New Jersey.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as an operator under New Jersey's law on student online education services (P.L.2019, c.494, N.J.S.A. 56:8-215 to 56:8-221, approved January 21, 2020): no targeted advertising, no sale or rental, no profile except for Kindergarten to Grade 12 school purposes, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), reasonable security, breach notice to the school without unreasonable delay (New Jersey's breach law, N.J.S.A. 56:8-163, asks for the most expedient time possible), and deletion when the school or district asks, or when a student who has reached the age of majority asks. A violation is an unlawful practice under the Consumer Fraud Act (56:8-220). FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- New Jersey: English language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit; the Amistad (African American history) and Holocaust and genocide mandates, and the diversity and inclusion law (P.L. 2021, c.32), have no unit of their own; Grade 9 social studies covers civics, economics and personal finance only; Lenape content is light and needs partner review (see `docs/research/new-jersey/README.md`).
