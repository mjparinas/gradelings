# New Jersey
- **Framework `us-nj`, slug `new-jersey`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the New Jersey Student Learning Standards (NJDOE): mathematics (2023; Common Core codes with cluster letters, `4.NBT.A.1`, with measurement and data split into `4.M.B.4` and Data Literacy `4.DL.A.1`, and money in Kindergarten and Grade 1; Grade 9 cites the high school codes, `A.SSE.A.1`), English language arts (2023; strand, sub-strand, grade and number, `RL.CR.4.1`, `RI.AA.4.7`, foundational skills under language, `L.RF.2.3`; Grade 9 cites the 9–10 band with NJDOE's en dash, `RI.CI.9–10.2`), science (NGSS codes, readopted June 2026; middle school placed like California's integrated model, `HS-` under Grade 9) and social studies (2020; `6.1.5.HistoryCC.3`, K-2 geography printed `6.1.2.Geo.SV.1`, Grades 6 to 8 split into US history `6.1.8.*` (eras 3 to 5) and world history `6.2.8.*` (eras 1 to 4), Grade 9 cites `6.1.12.*`), plus personal financial literacy from the Career Readiness, Life Literacies, and Key Skills standards (`9.1.5.PB.1`, `9.1.12.CDM.8`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`njs()` in `src/content/new-jersey/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/new-jersey/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; nj.gov allows scripted downloads; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `docs/research/new-jersey/README.md`).
- **Content:** math, English language arts and science follow California's placement with New Jersey codes (`src/content/new-jersey/plan/`), copying the same units from the same files (each grade file's `POOL`; money in Kindergarten and Grade 1 comes from New York's coin units); California's science units that name California places are replaced by `nj-` copies with New Jersey examples, and other borrowed units that name another state's places by filtered copies (`localCopy`, `units/copies-g*.ts`). Social studies follows the 2020 standards: general units in Kindergarten to Grade 3 with New Jersey's symbols (1) and its land, communities and local government (3), **New Jersey studies in Grade 4 (all New Jersey's own: the Lenape, the colony, the Revolution, a growing state, a timeline, state government and the economy)**, early US history (5), ancient world history (6), world history to about 1800 (7), US history from the founding through Reconstruction with New Jersey and the nation (8), and civics with New Jersey's three constitutions, the US government, personal finance and economics (9). New Jersey-only units have `nj-` ids (`bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/new-jersey/units/`. No borrowed unit may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`) outside US history and civics, and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`new-jersey.test.ts`). Lenape content is light, in the present tense, names the Ramapough Lenape and Nanticoke Lenni-Lenape as living communities, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; slavery and segregation in New Jersey are told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are the NJSLA (adaptive in English language arts and math since spring 2026; English language arts in grades 3 to 9, math in 3 to 8 and high school courses), NJSLA-Science (grades 5, 8 and 11) and the NJGPA graduation test in grade 11.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/new-jersey/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, the six Life Literacies and Key Skills (NJSLS-CLKS 9.4, not a separate graded subject; `/guides/new-jersey/life-literacies/`) and the state tests (`/guides/new-jersey/state-tests/`; dates point to NJDOE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our commitments under New Jersey's law on student online education services (P.L.2019, c.494, N.J.S.A. 56:8-215 to 56:8-221) next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **New Jersey.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as an operator under New Jersey's law on student online education services (P.L.2019, c.494, N.J.S.A. 56:8-215 to 56:8-221, approved January 21, 2020): no targeted advertising, no sale or rental, no profile except for Kindergarten to Grade 12 school purposes, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), reasonable security, breach notice to the school without unreasonable delay (New Jersey's breach law, N.J.S.A. 56:8-163, asks for the most expedient time possible), and deletion when the school or district asks, or when a student who has reached the age of majority asks. A violation is an unlawful practice under the Consumer Fraud Act (56:8-220). FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps

- New Jersey: English language arts standards that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit; the Amistad (African American history) and Holocaust and genocide mandates, and the diversity and inclusion law (P.L. 2021, c.32), have no unit of their own; Grade 9 social studies covers civics, economics and personal finance only; Lenape content is light and needs partner review (see `docs/research/new-jersey/README.md`).
