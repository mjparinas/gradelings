# Illinois
- **Framework `us-il`, slug `illinois`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Illinois Learning Standards (ISBE): Common Core math (`4.NBT.1`; Grade 9 cites the high school codes, `A-SSE.1`) and English language arts (`RL.4.1`; Grade 9 cites `RL.9-10.1`), NGSS science (2014; `MS-` under Grades 6 to 8, `HS-` under Grade 9) and the 2016 Social Science Standards (`SS.4.H.3`, `SS.K-2.IS.4`; Grades 6 to 8 share one band at three levels, `SS.6-8.H.1.LC`/`.MdC`/`.MC`; Grade 9 cites `SS.9-12.CV.4`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`ill()` in `src/content/illinois/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/illinois/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `gradelings-research/illinois/README.md`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), each citing the Illinois Early Learning and Development Standards (2013) preschool benchmarks (`6.A.ECb`, `31.C.ECa`; `src/content/illinois/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/illinois/prek/outcomes.json` (PDF in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** math, English language arts and science follow California's placement with Illinois codes (`src/content/illinois/plan/`), copying the same units from the same files (each grade file's `POOL`); California's science units that name California places are replaced by `il-` copies with Illinois examples (`units/science-g*.ts`), and other borrowed units that name another state's places by filtered copies (`localCopy` in `units/copies-g*.ts`). Social studies follows the grade themes (My Social World; Living, Learning, and Working Together; Families, Neighborhoods, and Communities; Communities Near and Far; **Our State, Our Nation in Grade 4, all Illinois's own**; Our Nation, Our World), then world geography and ancient civilizations (6), world history (7), United States history to Reconstruction with the Constitution test on the US and Illinois constitutions (8, 105 ILCS 5/27-3) and civics (9), with Illinois-only units (`il-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/illinois/units/`. No borrowed unit may name another state's places (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`) outside US history and civics, and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`illinois.test.ts`). Native content (Cahokia, the Illinois Confederation and the Peoria Tribe, Chicago's urban Native community) is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are the IAR (English language arts and math, grades 3 to 8), the Illinois Science Assessment (grades 5 and 8), PreACT 9 and PreACT, the ACT in grade 11, and the KIDS observation in kindergarten.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/illinois/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the Social and Emotional Learning Standards revised in July 2026 (five goals, not graded; `/guides/illinois/sel-standards/`) and the state tests (`/guides/illinois/state-tests/`; dates point to ISBE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Student Online Personal Protection Act (SOPPA, 105 ILCS 85) commitments next to the other states', including breach notice to the school within 30 days.

## Schools

- **Illinois.** `/privacy/` states our commitments as an operator under the Illinois Student Online Personal Protection Act (SOPPA, 105 ILCS 85, as amended in 2021): no targeted advertising, no sale or rental, no profile beyond the school's purposes, disclosure only as the law allows (the school and the listed providers, who are contractually bound not to use or disclose it further and to keep it secure), reasonable security, breach notice to the school within 30 calendar days of finding it, and deletion when the school asks. SOPPA has an Illinois school share covered information with an operator only under a written agreement, which the school must publish with a list of its operators; offer the district's own agreement or the NDPA with Illinois's exhibit if it asks. FERPA and COPPA apply as everywhere.

## Known gaps
