# Washington
- **Grade and region wording:** `gradeLabel` (`src/lib/gradeLabel.ts` in the apps, `src/components/site/curriculum.ts` on public pages) gives "Grade 4" in Canada and "4th grade" in the US, and pickers group provinces and states by country (`src/lib/frameworkGroups.ts`, `byCountry` in `src/components/site/provinces.ts`). Never type "province" or "Grade n" where a US family could read it. The parent area and progress emails are in US English for a US family: wrap parent copy in `say(...)` (`useSay` in `src/lib/say.ts`; `PageTitle`, `Panel`, `Switch` and `Choice` already do), format dates with `useLocale()`, and pass the family's `country` to email templates. `src/components/parents/usEnglish.test.tsx` renders each parent page for a US family and fails on Canadian spelling, "Grade n" or "province". The kids' app does the same for a US child with `useChildSay()` (home, streak line, quests, trophies, toasts, shop, My Room, lesson feedback); `src/components/play/usEnglish.test.tsx` checks its screens.
- **Framework `us-wa`, slug `washington`**, Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French; Spanish may come later). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, so pickers, public pages and the sitemap show it. `UPCOMING_FRAMEWORKS` stays exported, empty, for the next state while it is built. Plan doc: the Washington plan (owner-approved October 2026: US$14.99/US$119.99, Grade 9 social studies is civics, one site for both countries, classroom mode at launch).
- **Standards** are OSPI's: the 2026 math and ELA standards (`M.4.Q.NBT.2`, `ELA.4.R.3`; Grade 9 cites `M.HS…` and `ELA.9-10.…`), NGSS for science (`4-PS3-2`, `MS-LS1-1`, `HS-…`) and the 2019 social studies standards (`H1.4.2`, `C3.9-10.1`). A citation is `"CODES · plain text"` (`wa()` in `src/content/washington/cite.ts`); its `contract.ts` checks every code against `docs/research/washington/outcomes.json` (rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`).
- **US English layer** (`src/content/locale.ts`): Canadian units are shown in US spelling with comma-grouped numbers, and WHMIS as GHS, at view time (`localizeUnit`), so a shared unit keeps one id and progress. `canadianWords` keeps units about Canada (and Canadian money, tax or BEDMAS) out of US courses; Washington-only units are exempt because they are written for the US. Money questions in a US course must carry `currency: "USD"` (`src/content/money.ts`).
- **Content:** BC units via `share`, other provinces' units via `adopt` (`src/content/washington/plan/`), and Washington-only units in `src/content/washington/units/` (`wa-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`): US money and customary units, data science, Algebra 1, Research and Media Literacy, NGSS middle school (6 matter, cells, weather; 7 reactions, ecosystems, Earth; 8 forces, heredity, space), and almost all social studies (Grade 4 and 7 Washington State, 5 and 8 US history, 6 world, 9 civics). Tribal content is light and in the present tense. Each grade file passes only its own grade's units (`units/own-<grade>.ts`), so a grade's download doesn't carry the others; Oregon and California do the same. Each Washington, Oregon and California grade file also lists, per subject, the exact unit files it copies from (`POOL`, a `Pool` in `placement.ts`) instead of importing other frameworks' whole grades; when a new `adopt` entry fails with "no unit … to adopt", import the file that defines the unit there.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale, so four practice steps (Beginning, Approaching, Meeting, Exceeding), like Alberta.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/washington/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Kindergarten to Grade 9, OSPI's six Social Emotional Learning standards (`/guides/washington/sel-standards/`; they are not graded) and the state tests (`/guides/washington/state-tests/`: Smarter Balanced in English language arts and math in grades 3 to 8, WCAS science in grades 5 and 8; the schedule changes, so the guide points to OSPI). No French guide. The report-card page is `/report-cards/washington/`.
