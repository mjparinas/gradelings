# South Carolina
- **Framework `us-sc`, slug `south-carolina`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the South Carolina College- and Career-Ready Standards (SCDE): mathematics (2025; `4.NR.1.2`, strands NR, PAFR, MGSR and DPSR; Grade 9 is Algebra 1, `A1.PAFR.2.3`), English language arts (2023; `ELA.4.AOR.1.2`, `ELA.2.F.3.4`; Grade 9 is English 1, `ELA.E1.AOR.2.1`), science (2021; NGSS performance expectations placed by grade, middle school included, `6-ESS2-1`, `8-PS2-1`; Grade 9 cites Biology 1, `B-LS1-1`, with parts of Chemistry and Physics) and social studies (2019; `K.H.1`, `3.1.2.AG`, `4.1.CO`, `7.2.3.HS`; Grade 9 cites United States Government, `USG.2.CC`, and Economics and Personal Finance, `EPF.3.IP`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`sca()` in `src/content/south-carolina/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/south-carolina/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`; ed.sc.gov allows scripted downloads; rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record and gaps: `docs/research/south-carolina/README.md`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), cited by the South Carolina Early Learning Standards' Older Preschoolers indicators (`MTE-1z`) (`src/content/south-carolina/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/south-carolina/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** BC units via `share`; other states' and the provinces' units via `adopt` (`src/content/south-carolina/plan/`; pools in `pools/<subject>-<grade>.ts`, `southCarolinaGrade` in `kit.ts`), plus South Carolina-only units (`sc-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`, copies with `copyOf` and `localCopy` in `units/kit.ts`) in `src/content/south-carolina/units/`. Math follows Arkansas's and California's placement and English language arts California's, re-cited to South Carolina's indicators; science follows South Carolina's own grade placement. Borrowed units that name another state's places are filtered `sc-` copies; `south-carolina.test.ts` checks no other borrowed unit names another state's places outside US history and civics (`OTHER_STATE` in `units/kit.ts`), and that no other state's Tribal units are borrowed.
- **Social studies** follows the 2019 standards' grade themes: the community around us (K), **Life in South Carolina (1)**, Life in the United States (2), World Geography (3), **United States and South Carolina Studies I and II (4 and 5**, with South Carolina's own units on the colony, the Revolution, the Civil War, Reconstruction, mills to ports and civil rights), World Civilizations (6), World Regional Geography (7), **South Carolina and the United States (8, mostly South Carolina's own units)** and United States Government with Economics and Personal Finance (9, with South Carolina's government). Catawba Indian Nation and Gullah Geechee content is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial; slavery, the Stono Rebellion, Jim Crow and the Orangeburg Massacre are told plainly.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale for Kindergarten to Grade 8 (high school credit courses use the Uniform Grading Policy), so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are SC READY (English language arts and math, grades 3 to 8), SCPASS (science) and end-of-course exams (Algebra 1, English 2, Biology 1, US History); the Read to Succeed 3rd grade reading rule is on the state tests guide.
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/south-carolina/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the Profile of the South Carolina Graduate (`/guides/south-carolina/graduate-profile/`, not graded) and the state tests (`/guides/south-carolina/state-tests/`; dates point to SCDE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** South Carolina has no student online privacy statute for technology providers, so `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` give South Carolina schools the same commitments as the other states', with breach notice to the school straight away (S.C. Code 39-1-90 asks a business holding data for another to tell it immediately following discovery). Keep that true.

## Schools

- **South Carolina.** South Carolina has no student online privacy statute binding technology providers. Section 59-1-490 of the South Carolina Code sets the Department of Education's data use and governance policy and has districts keep their own student data policies and minimum cybersecurity safeguards, so a South Carolina district will ask for its own agreement or the NDPA. `/privacy/` gives South Carolina schools the same commitments as the other states', under the school's agreement and FERPA and COPPA. South Carolina's breach notification law (S.C. Code 39-1-90) has a business that keeps data for another tell its owner of a breach immediately following discovery; `/privacy/` promises the school notice straight away.

## Known gaps

- South Carolina: English language arts indicators that need writing, speaking or fluency have no unit; Grade 9 science covers Biology 1 and only parts of Chemistry and Physics; Economics and Personal Finance is covered lightly; Catawba and Gullah Geechee content is light and needs partner review (see `docs/research/south-carolina/README.md`).
