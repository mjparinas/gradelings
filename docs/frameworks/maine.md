# Maine
- **Framework `us-me`, slug `maine`**, Pre-K and Kindergarten to Grade 9 in math, English language arts, science and social studies (no French). It is in `FRAMEWORKS`, offered to families and in classroom mode, like the other states. Standards are the Maine Learning Results (Maine DOE): mathematics 2020 (Common Core codes with cluster letters, `4.NBT.A.1`; Grade 9 cites the high school codes, `HSA.SSE.A.1`; Maine adds `1.MD.D.5`, coins), English language arts 2020 (no codes: standards are named, `Reading 4`, `Writing 2`, `Language 3`, `Speaking and Listening 1`; Grades 6–8 and 9–Diploma are spans), science and engineering 2019 (NGSS codes; middle school is one span, placed like California's integrated model) and social studies 2019 (named expectations, `Civics & Government 2`, `Geography 1`, `History 2`, `Personal Finance`, `Economics`, `Global Connections`). Citations are `"CODES · text"` (`mai()` in `src/content/maine/cite.ts`), checked by its `contract.ts` against `docs/research/maine/outcomes.json` (rebuild with `fetch.sh` and `build_outcomes.py`; record: `gradelings-research/maine/README.md`).
- **Pre-K:** the 26 shared Pre-K units (`src/content/grades/pk/`), each citing a preschool statement of the Maine Early Learning and Development Standards (`CC.P.3`, `SD.P.6`; the Common Standards Project's copy, as maine.gov refuses scripted downloads) (`src/content/maine/pk.ts`), checked against `docs/research/maine/prek/outcomes.json` (sources in `source/`, `fetch.sh`, `build_outcomes.py`). Practice steps only.
- **Content:** math, English language arts and science follow California's placement with Maine citations (`src/content/maine/plan/`), copying the same units from the same files (each grade file's `POOL`) but never California's own `ca-` science copies. Social studies adopts general units from BC, the provinces, Washington, Oregon and California, plus Maine-only units (`me-` ids, `bankUnit` in `own.ts`) in `src/content/maine/units/`: Grade 1 symbols and Wabanaki traditions; Grade 3 Maine's land, towns and communities; Grade 4 Maine studies (map, history, timeline, government, economy, the Wabanaki Nations); Grade 8 Maine and the nation to Reconstruction; Grade 9 governing Maine, the US government, personal finance and economics. No unit Maine uses may name Washington State, Oregon or California as a place (US history mentions of California are fine), and other states' Tribal units are never borrowed (`maine.test.ts`). Wabanaki content (Penobscot Nation, Passamaquoddy Tribe, Houlton Band of Maliseet Indians, Mi'kmaq Nation) is light, in the present tense, with nothing sacred or ceremonial.
- **Report card:** no statewide scale (many schools use standards-based 1 to 4 reporting), so the same four practice steps as the other states. State tests are MeCAS: the Maine Through Year Assessment (math and reading, grades 3 to 8 and the second year of high school, fall and spring with an optional winter window) and the Maine Science Assessment (grades 5 and 8 and the third year of high school).
- **Parent guides** (`src/content/maine/guides.ts`, US English): the hub, grade notes for Pre-K to Grade 9, the five Guiding Principles of the Maine Learning Results (`/guides/maine/guiding-principles/`, not graded on their own) and the state tests (`/guides/maine/state-tests/`; dates point to the Maine DOE). No French guide.
- **Schools:** `/privacy/` and `docs/school/` state our Maine Student Information Privacy Act (20-A M.R.S. sections 951 to 953) commitments next to the other states'.

## Schools

- **Maine law.** `/privacy/` states the matching commitments under Maine's Student Information Privacy Act (20-A M.R.S. sections 951 to 953): no targeted advertising, no sale, no profiling beyond the school's purpose, sharing only as the law allows, a security program and deletion when the school asks.

## Known gaps

- Maine: Wabanaki content is light and needs partner review before it grows, and Personal Finance and Economics are practised mainly in Grades 4 and 9 (see `gradelings-research/maine/README.md`).
